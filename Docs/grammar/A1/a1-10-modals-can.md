# A1-10 Modals: can / can't / could

## Goal
You can express ability, permission and requests with can, can't and could.

## Rule in 60 seconds
can + yalın fiil: `I can swim.` Soru: `Can you swim?` Olumsuz: can't.
can her özneyle aynı kalır, -s almaz: She can swim.
could = can'in geçmişi (I could swim when I was 5) + kibar rica (Could you help?).
İzin: `Can I go out?` Rica: `Can/Could you open the window?`
Yasak A1'de can't ile: `You can't smoke here.`
Gelecek planında da can kullanılır: `I can meet you tomorrow.`
Teklif kalıbı: `Can I help you?` / `Can I carry that?`
Yetenek sorusu: `Can you swim?` — cevap hep can/can't ile.
İzin + yasak ikilisi: `Can I park here? — No, you can't.`
Kibarlık sırası: Could you...? > Can you...? > ...please!
Cevap + alternatif: `No, I can't, but she can.`

## Forms

| Form | Pattern | Examples |
|------|---------|----------|
| + Ability | S + can + V | I **can swim**. / She **can drive**. |
| − Ability | S + can't + V | He **can't fly**. / They **can't come**. |
| ? Ability | Can + S + V? | **Can** you swim? / **Can** she drive? |
| Permission | Can + I ...? | **Can** I go out? / **Can** I use your pen? |
| Request | Can/Could + you ...? | **Can** you help me? / **Could** you open it? |
| Prohibition | You + can't + V | You **can't smoke** here. |
| Past ability | S + could + V | I **could swim** when I was five. |
| Past − | S + couldn't + V | He **couldn't** walk last year. |
| Short answer | Yes, S + can. / No, S + can't. | Yes, I **can**. / No, she **can't**. |
| Know-how | can + V (beceri) | She **can cook** well. / He **can speak** Turkish. |

can't = cannot. couldn't = could not.
could sorusu daha kibar: Could you...? > Can you...?

## 3 classic traps

1. **can'den sonra to yok, fiile -s yok.**
   - ✗ She can to swim. / She cans swim. → ✓ She **can swim**.
   - ✗ He can plays chess. → ✓ He **can play** chess.
   - can her zaman + yalın fiil, özne fark etmez.
2. **Soru sırası: Can başa gelir, do yok.**
   - ✗ Do you can swim? → ✓ **Can** you swim?
   - ✗ You can swim? (yazıda) → ✓ **Can** you swim?
   - can kendi başına yardımcı fiildir, do/does almaz.
3. **could ≠ şimdiki yetenek (genelde).**
   - ✗ I could swim very well. (şimdi) → ✓ I **can** swim very well.
   - could = geçmiş yetenek ya da kibar rica.
   - Şimdi yapabiliyorsan can, çocukken yapabiliyorsan could.

## 10 example sentences

1. I **can swim**. — Yüzebilirim.
2. She **can't drive**. — Araba kullanamaz.
3. **Can** you help me? — Bana yardım edebilir misin?
4. **Can** I use your phone? — Telefonunu kullanabilir miyim?
5. You **can't** eat here. — Burada yiyemezsin. (yasak)
6. I **could** read when I was four. — Dört yaşındayken okuyabiliyordum.
7. **Could** you open the window, please? — Pencereyi açar mısın lütfen? (kibar)
8. He **couldn't** walk after the accident. — Kazadan sonra yürüyemedi.
9. Yes, I **can**. / No, I **can't**. — Evet, yapabilirim. / Hayır, yapamam.
10. They **can** speak three languages. — Üç dil konuşabilirler.

## Quick check

1. Correct: She cans play tennis.
<details><summary>Answer</summary>She <b>can play</b> tennis. (can değişmez + yalın fiil)</details>

2. Make a polite request: you / open / the door.
<details><summary>Answer</summary><b>Could</b> you open the door, please? (Can de olur, could daha kibar)</details>

3. Past or present? "He ___ walk when he was one." (can / could)
<details><summary>Answer</summary>He <b>could</b> walk when he was one. (geçmiş yetenek)</details>
