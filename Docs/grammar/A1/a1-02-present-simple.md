# A1-02 Present Simple

## Goal

You can talk about habits, routines and facts with the present simple.

## Rule in 60 seconds

- **Kural 1:** Geniş zaman: I/you/we/they + V (I work), he/she/it + V-**s** (she works).

- **Kural 2:** Olumsuz: don't / doesn't + V. Soru: Do / Does + özne + V?

- **Kural 3:** Sıklık zarfları (always, usually, often, sometimes, never) fiilden önce gelir.

- **Kural 4:** Ama be fiilinden sonra gelir: `She is always late.`

- **Kural 5:** 3. tekil şahıs kuralı sadece olumlu cümlede -s ister.

- **Kural 6:** Olumsuz ve soruda -s düşer çünkü işareti do/does taşır.

- **Kural 7:** Rutin zaman ifadeleri: every day, on Mondays, twice a week.

- **Kural 8:** Genel gerçekler de bu zamandadır: `Water boils at 100 degrees.`

- **Kural 9:** have fiili 3. tekilde has olur: `She has breakfast at 8.`

- **Kural 10:** Olumsuz soruda do/does + not sırası: `Why doesn't he come?`

- **Kural 11:** Kısa cevapta fiil tekrarlanmaz: `Do you swim? Yes, I do.`

## Forms

| Form | Pattern | Examples |
|------|---------|----------|
| + Positive | I/you/we/they + V | I **work** here. / They **live** in İzmir. |
| + Positive | he/she/it + V-s | She **works** here. / He **lives** in İzmir. |
| + Positive | -s spelling | go→**goes**, do→**does**, study→**studies** |
| + Positive | -s spelling | watch→**watches**, have→**has**, fly→**flies** |
| − Negative | S + don't/doesn't + V | I **don't** smoke. / She **doesn't** smoke. |
| − Negative | S + don't/doesn't + V | We **don't** watch TV. / It **doesn't** rain much. |
| ? Question | Do/Does + S + V? | **Do** you like tea? / **Does** he like tea? |
| ? Wh-question | Wh + do/does + S + V? | **Where** do you work? / **What** does she do? |
| Short answer | Yes, S + do/does. | Yes, I **do**. / Yes, she **does**. |
| Frequency | S + adv + V | I **always** drink tea. / She **never** eats meat. |

- **Not 1:** does + he/she/it ile kullanılır; do diğer öznelerle.

- **Not 2:** never olumsuz anlam taşır, yanında don't kullanılmaz.

## 3 classic traps

Bu bölümdeki 3 tuzağa tek bakışta dikkat et.

- **3. tekil şahısta -s unutma:** en klasik hata — ✗ He go to work. → ✓ He **goes** to work; ✗ She like apples. → ✓ She **likes** apples — Kontrol: özne he/she/it mi, Evet → fiile -s.

- **doesn't'tan sonra -s yok:** ek bir kere gelir — ✗ She doesn't likes coffee. → ✓ She **doesn't like** coffee; ✗ He doesn't goes out. → ✓ He **doesn't go** out — Mantık: doesn't zaten -s işaretini taşıyor (does = do + es).

- **Soruda da -s yok:** Does zaten işareti taşır — ✗ Does she works here? → ✓ **Does** she **work** here; ✗ Do he play chess? → ✓ **Does** he play chess — Do/does seçimi özneye göre, fiil hep yalın.

## 10 example sentences

Her cümleyi sesli oku, önce İngilizceyi sonra Türkçesini söyle.

- **I get up at 7 every day.** — Her gün 7'de kalkarım.

- **She goes to school by bus.** — O okula otobüsle gider.

- **They don't eat meat.** — Onlar et yemez.

- **He doesn't speak English.** — O İngilizce konuşmaz.

- **Do you work here?** — Burada mı çalışıyorsun?

- **Does your mother cook well?** — Annen iyi yemek yapar mı?

- **Water boils at 100 degrees.** — Su 100 derecede kaynar. (fact).

- **I always drink tea in the morning.** — Sabahları hep çay içerim.

- **She never watches horror films.** — O hiç korku filmi izlemez.

- **We have breakfast together on Sundays.** — Pazarları birlikte kahvaltı yaparız.
