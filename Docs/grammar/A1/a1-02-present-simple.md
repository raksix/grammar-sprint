# A1-02 Present Simple

## Goal
You can talk about habits, routines and facts with the present simple.

## Rule in 60 seconds
Geniş zaman: I/you/we/they + V (I work), he/she/it + V-**s** (she works).
Olumsuz: don't / doesn't + V. Soru: Do / Does + özne + V?
Sıklık zarfları (always, usually, often, sometimes, never) fiilden önce gelir.
Ama be fiilinden sonra gelir: `She is always late.`
3. tekil şahıs kuralı sadece olumlu cümlede -s ister.
Olumsuz ve soruda -s düşer çünkü işareti do/does taşır.
Rutin zaman ifadeleri: every day, on Mondays, twice a week.
Genel gerçekler de bu zamandadır: `Water boils at 100 degrees.`
have fiili 3. tekilde has olur: `She has breakfast at 8.`
Olumsuz soruda do/does + not sırası: `Why doesn't he come?`
Kısa cevapta fiil tekrarlanmaz: `Do you swim? Yes, I do.`

## Forms

| Form | Pattern | Examples |
|------|---------|----------|
| + Positive | I/you/we/they + V | I **work** here. / They **live** in İzmir. |
| + Positive | he/she/it + V-s | She **works** here. / He **lives** in İzmir. |
| + Positive | -s spelling | go→**goes**, do→**does**, study→**studies** |
| + Positive | -s spelling | watch→**watches**, have→**has**, fly→**flies** |
| − Negative | S + don't/doesn't + V | I **don't** smoke. / She **doesn't** smoke. |
| − Negative | S + don't/doesn't + V | We **don't** watch TV. / It **doesn't** rain much. |
| ? Question | Do/Does + S + V? | **Do** you like tea? / **Does** he like tea? |
| ? Wh-question | Wh + do/does + S + V? | **Where** do you work? / **What** does she do? |
| Short answer | Yes, S + do/does. | Yes, I **do**. / Yes, she **does**. |
| Frequency | S + adv + V | I **always** drink tea. / She **never** eats meat. |

does + he/she/it ile kullanılır; do diğer öznelerle.
never olumsuz anlam taşır, yanında don't kullanılmaz.

## 3 classic traps

1. **3. tekil şahısta -s unutma** — en klasik hata.
   - ✗ He go to work. → ✓ He **goes** to work.
   - ✗ She like apples. → ✓ She **likes** apples.
   - Kontrol: özne he/she/it mi? Evet → fiile -s.
2. **doesn't'tan sonra -s yok** — ek bir kere gelir.
   - ✗ She doesn't likes coffee. → ✓ She **doesn't like** coffee.
   - ✗ He doesn't goes out. → ✓ He **doesn't go** out.
   - Mantık: doesn't zaten -s işaretini taşıyor (does = do + es).
3. **Soruda da -s yok** — Does zaten işareti taşır.
   - ✗ Does she works here? → ✓ **Does** she **work** here?
   - ✗ Do he play chess? → ✓ **Does** he play chess?
   - Do/does seçimi özneye göre, fiil hep yalın.

## 10 example sentences

1. I **get up** at 7 every day. — Her gün 7'de kalkarım.
2. She **goes** to school by bus. — O okula otobüsle gider.
3. They **don't** eat meat. — Onlar et yemez.
4. He **doesn't** speak English. — O İngilizce konuşmaz.
5. **Do** you work here? — Burada mı çalışıyorsun?
6. **Does** your mother cook well? — Annen iyi yemek yapar mı?
7. Water **boils** at 100 degrees. — Su 100 derecede kaynar. (fact)
8. I **always** drink tea in the morning. — Sabahları hep çay içerim.
9. She **never** watches horror films. — O hiç korku filmi izlemez.
10. We **have** breakfast together on Sundays. — Pazarları birlikte kahvaltı yaparız.

1. Fill in: He ___ (play) football on Saturdays.
