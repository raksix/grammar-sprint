# A1-09 Prepositions (in / on / at + movement)

## Goal
You can use in, on, at for place and time, plus basic movement prepositions.

## Rule in 60 seconds
YER: in (içinde: in a box), on (üstünde: on the table), at (nokta: at school).
ZAMAN: in (ay/yıl/mevsim: in May), on (gün: on Monday), at (saat: at 5).
Hareket: go to (yön), come from (kaynak), get on/off, go into/out of.
Sıralı ezber: ay→in, gün→on, saat→at (in May, on Monday, at 7).
Ev/iş kalıpları: at home, at work, at school — the yok.
Taşıt: arabada in, büyük taşıtta on: in a car, on the bus.
Adres: at + kapı no (at 5 Park Street), on + cadde (on Park Street).
Evde/dışarıda: at home vs outside — out of binadan çıkış.
Sınıf içi konum: in the front row, at the back of the class.
Zaman aralığı: from Monday to Friday, between 9 and 5.
Bayram/gün adı on ile: on New Year's Day, on my birthday.

## Forms

| Form | Pattern | Examples |
|------|---------|----------|
| place: in | in + closed space / area | **in** the box / **in** Ankara / **in** bed |
| place: on | on + surface | **on** the table / **on** the wall |
| place: at | at + point / building as point | **at** school / **at** home / **at** the door |
| time: in | in + month/year/season/part of day | **in** May / **in** 2024 / **in** the morning |
| time: on | on + day/date | **on** Monday / **on** 5 May |
| time: at | at + clock/night/weekend | **at** 7 o'clock / **at** night / **at** the weekend |
| movement | to / from / into / out of | go **to** school / come **from** work |
| movement | up / down / across / along / past | go **up** the stairs / walk **past** the bank |
| transport | in + car/taxi, on + bus/train/plane | **in** a taxi / **on** the bus |
| fixed | by + transport, on + foot | **by** bus / **on** foot |

next/last/every ile edat yok: next Monday, last night, every morning.
in the evening ama at night — istisnayı ezberle.

## 3 classic traps

1. **Gün = on, saat = at, ay = in.**
   - ✗ in Monday, at May → ✓ **on** Monday, **in** May.
   - ✗ on 7 o'clock → ✓ **at** 7 o'clock.
   - Üçlüyü cümleyle ezberle: at 5 on Monday in May.
2. **at home'da the yok.**
   - ✗ at the home → ✓ **at home**.
   - Aynı kalıp: at work, at school, in bed, to bed.
   - Ama bina olaraksa the gelir: in the school building.
3. **to'dan sonra fiil değil isim gelir.**
   - ✗ I go to play. (yön yoksa) → ✓ I go **to** school. / I want **to** play.
   - to + yer ismi (yön), to + fiil (amaç) — ikisi farklı to.
   - from eşleşmesi: from ... to ... (from Ankara to İzmir).

## 10 example sentences

1. The keys are **on** the table. — Anahtarlar masanın üstünde.
2. I live **in** Ankara. — Ankara'da yaşıyorum.
3. She is **at** school now. — O şu an okulda.
4. My birthday is **in** May. — Doğum günüm Mayıs'ta.
5. We meet **on** Mondays. — Pazartesileri buluşuruz.
6. The bus leaves **at** 7 o'clock. — Otobüs saat 7'de kalkar.
7. He goes **to** work **by** bus. — İşe otobüsle gider.
8. She came **from** İzmir yesterday. — Dün İzmir'den geldi.
9. The cat is **under** the chair. — Kedi sandalyenin altında.
10. Walk **past** the bank, then turn left. — Bankayı geç, sonra sola dön.

## Quick check

1. Fill in: The meeting is ___ Monday ___ 10 o'clock. (in / on / at)
<details><summary>Answer</summary>The meeting is <b>on</b> Monday <b>at</b> 10 o'clock.</details>

2. Correct: I live at Ankara.
<details><summary>Answer</summary>I live <b>in</b> Ankara. (şehir → in)</details>

3. Choose: She goes to school (by / on) foot vs bus?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>She goes to school <b>on</b> foot / <b>by</b> bus.</details>
