# A1-03 Present Continuous

## Goal

You can describe what is happening right now with am/is/are + V-ing.

## Rule in 60 seconds

- **Kural 1:** Şu an olan iş: be + fiil-**ing** (I am working).

- **Kural 2:** Sinyal kelimeler: now, right now, at the moment, Listen!, Look!

- **Kural 3:** Geniş zaman (alışkanlık) ile karıştırma: every day → simple, now → continuous.

- **Kural 4:** be fiilini atma: `I working` yanlıştır, `I am working` doğrudur.

- **Kural 5:** -ing yazımı: make→making, run→running, lie→lying, die→dying.

- **Kural 6:** Bazı fiiller (know, like, want, need, have=possess) genelde -ing almaz.

- **Kural 7:** Soru: be başa gelir — `Are you listening?`

- **Kural 8:** Olumsuz: be + not + V-ing — `She isn't working.`

- **Kural 9:** Zaman çizgisi ipucu: şimdi + geçici durum = continuous.

- **Kural 10:** Yarınki kesin plan da continuous olur: `We are meeting at 6.`

- **Kural 11:** Olumsuz soru tonu: `Aren't you coming?` (şaşkınlık / davet).

- **Kural 12:** Cevap kalıbı: `What are you doing? — I'm cooking.`

## Forms

| Form | Pattern | Examples |
|------|---------|----------|
| + Positive | S + am/is/are + V-ing | I **am reading**. / She **is reading**. |
| + Positive | S + am/is/are + V-ing | They **are playing** football now. |
| + Positive | future plan (A1 intro) | I **am meeting** Ali tomorrow. |
| − Negative | S + am/is/are + not + V-ing | I**'m not sleeping**. / He **isn't working**. |
| − Negative | S + am/is/are + not + V-ing | We **aren't watching** TV. |
| ? Question | Am/Is/Are + S + V-ing? | **Are** you **listening**? / **Is** she **coming**? |
| ? Wh-question | Wh + am/is/are + S + V-ing? | **What** are you **doing**? |
| Short answer + | Yes, S + am/is/are. | Yes, I **am**. / Yes, she **is**. |
| Short answer − | No, S + 'm not/isn't/aren't. | No, he **isn't**. / No, they **aren't**. |
| Spelling | drop -e / double consonant | mak**e**→making, run→run**ning**, swim→swim**ming** |

- **Not 1:** Stative verbs (A1): know, like, love, hate, want, need, have got.

- **Not 2:** Bunlarla present simple kullan: `I like it.` (değil: I am liking it).

## 3 classic traps

Bu bölümdeki 3 tuzağa tek bakışta dikkat et.

- **be'yi atma:** sadece -ing yetmez — ✗ I working now. → ✓ I **am working** now; ✗ They playing outside. → ✓ They **are playing** outside — -ing tek başına cümle kurmaz, yardımcı fiil şart.

- **Simple ile karıştırma:** now varken -s takısı olmaz — ✗ She works now. → ✓ She **is working** now — İpucu: cümlede now / Look! / Listen! varsa continuous; every day / always varsa simple: She works every day.

- **Stative fiillerde -ing yok (A1 seviyesinde):** know, like, want, need — ✗ I am knowing the answer. → ✓ I **know** the answer; ✗ She is liking pizza. → ✓ She **likes** pizza — Duygu, düşünce, sahiplik bildiren fiiller genelde simple kalır.

## 10 example sentences

Her cümleyi sesli oku, önce İngilizceyi sonra Türkçesini söyle.

- **I am studying English now.** — Şu an İngilizce çalışıyorum.

- **She is cooking dinner at the moment.** — Şu an akşam yemeği pişiriyor.

- **They are playing in the garden.** — Bahçede oynuyorlar.

- **Look! It is raining.** — Bak! Yağmur yağıyor.

- **Listen! The baby is crying.** — Dinle! Bebek ağlıyor.

- **We aren't watching TV now.** — Şu an TV izlemiyoruz.

- **He isn't sleeping, he is reading.** — O uyumuyor, kitap okuyor.

- **Are you coming with us?** — Bizimle geliyor musun?

- **Is your father working today?** — Baban bugün çalışıyor mu?

- **I work in a bank, but today I am working from home.** — Bankada çalışırım ama bugün evden çalışıyorum.

## Quick check

1. Fill in: Look! The cat ___ (sleep) on my bed.
<details><summary>Answer</summary>The cat <b>is sleeping</b> on my bed.</details>

2. Present simple or continuous? "She ___ (read) a book every evening."
<details><summary>Answer</summary>Present simple: She <b>reads</b> a book every evening. (every evening → rutin)</details>

3. Correct the error: They eating dinner now.
<details><summary>Answer</summary>They <b>are eating</b> dinner now. (be eklendi)</details>
