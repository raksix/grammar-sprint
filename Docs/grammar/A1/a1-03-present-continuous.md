# A1-03 Present Continuous

## Goal
You can describe what is happening right now with am/is/are + V-ing.

## Rule in 60 seconds
Şu an olan iş: be + fiil-**ing** (I am working).
Sinyal kelimeler: now, right now, at the moment, Listen!, Look!
Geniş zaman (alışkanlık) ile karıştırma: every day → simple, now → continuous.
be fiilini atma: `I working` yanlıştır, `I am working` doğrudur.
-ing yazımı: make→making, run→running, lie→lying, die→dying.
Bazı fiiller (know, like, want, need, have=possess) genelde -ing almaz.
Soru: be başa gelir — `Are you listening?`
Olumsuz: be + not + V-ing — `She isn't working.`
Zaman çizgisi ipucu: şimdi + geçici durum = continuous.
Yarınki kesin plan da continuous olur: `We are meeting at 6.`
Olumsuz soru tonu: `Aren't you coming?` (şaşkınlık / davet).
Cevap kalıbı: `What are you doing? — I'm cooking.`

## Forms

| Form | Pattern | Examples |
|------|---------|----------|
| + Positive | S + am/is/are + V-ing | I **am reading**. / She **is reading**. |
| + Positive | S + am/is/are + V-ing | They **are playing** football now. |
| + Positive | future plan (A1 intro) | I **am meeting** Ali tomorrow. |
| − Negative | S + am/is/are + not + V-ing | I**'m not sleeping**. / He **isn't working**. |
| − Negative | S + am/is/are + not + V-ing | We **aren't watching** TV. |
| ? Question | Am/Is/Are + S + V-ing? | **Are** you **listening**? / **Is** she **coming**? |
| ? Wh-question | Wh + am/is/are + S + V-ing? | **What** are you **doing**? |
| Short answer + | Yes, S + am/is/are. | Yes, I **am**. / Yes, she **is**. |
| Short answer − | No, S + 'm not/isn't/aren't. | No, he **isn't**. / No, they **aren't**. |
| Spelling | drop -e / double consonant | mak**e**→making, run→run**ning**, swim→swim**ming** |

Stative verbs (A1): know, like, love, hate, want, need, have got.
Bunlarla present simple kullan: `I like it.` (değil: I am liking it).

## 3 classic traps

1. **be'yi atma** — sadece -ing yetmez.
   - ✗ I working now. → ✓ I **am working** now.
   - ✗ They playing outside. → ✓ They **are playing** outside.
   - -ing tek başına cümle kurmaz, yardımcı fiil şart.
2. **Simple ile karıştırma** — now varken -s takısı olmaz.
   - ✗ She works now. → ✓ She **is working** now.
   - İpucu: cümlede now / Look! / Listen! varsa continuous.
   - every day / always varsa simple: She works every day.
3. **Stative fiillerde -ing yok (A1 seviyesinde)** — know, like, want, need.
   - ✗ I am knowing the answer. → ✓ I **know** the answer.
   - ✗ She is liking pizza. → ✓ She **likes** pizza.
   - Duygu, düşünce, sahiplik bildiren fiiller genelde simple kalır.

## 10 example sentences

1. I **am studying** English now. — Şu an İngilizce çalışıyorum.
2. She **is cooking** dinner at the moment. — Şu an akşam yemeği pişiriyor.
3. They **are playing** in the garden. — Bahçede oynuyorlar.
4. Look! It **is raining**. — Bak! Yağmur yağıyor.
5. Listen! The baby **is crying**. — Dinle! Bebek ağlıyor.
6. We **aren't watching** TV now. — Şu an TV izlemiyoruz.
7. He **isn't sleeping**, he is reading. — O uyumuyor, kitap okuyor.
8. **Are** you **coming** with us? — Bizimle geliyor musun?
9. **Is** your father **working** today? — Baban bugün çalışıyor mu?
10. I **work** in a bank, but today I **am working** from home. — Bankada çalışırım ama bugün evden çalışıyorum.

## Quick check

1. Fill in: Look! The cat ___ (sleep) on my bed.
<details><summary>Answer</summary>The cat <b>is sleeping</b> on my bed.</details>

2. Present simple or continuous? "She ___ (read) a book every evening."
<details><summary>Answer</summary>Present simple: She <b>reads</b> a book every evening. (every evening → rutin)</details>

3. Correct the error: They eating dinner now.
<details><summary>Answer</summary>They <b>are eating</b> dinner now. (be eklendi)</details>
