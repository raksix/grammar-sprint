# A1-05 Articles (a / an / the / zero)

## Goal

You can choose correctly between a, an, the or nothing before a noun.

## Rule in 60 seconds

- **Kural 1:** İlk bahsetme: **a/an** (I saw a cat). Tekrar bahsetme: **the** (The cat is black).

- **Kural 2:** an + sesli harf SESİ (an apple, an hour, an umbrella).

- **Kural 3:** Herkesin bildiği tek şey: **the** (the sun, the moon, the internet).

- **Kural 4:** Mesleklerde a/an şart: `She is a teacher.`

- **Kural 5:** Genel çoğul ve soyut isimlerde sıfır artikel: Cats are cute. / I like music.

- **Kural 6:** Yemekler, diller, sporlar, çoğu özel isim: sıfır artikel.

- **Kural 7:** the + sıfat = grup: the rich, the young, the Turkish.

- **Kural 8:** Gazete/otel/süperlatif genelde the ister: the news, the Hilton, the first.

- **Kural 9:** same her zaman the ile: `the same book`, `the same day.`

- **Kural 10:** Enstrümanlarda the: `play the piano`, `play the guitar`.

- **Kural 11:** Televizyon genel anlamda -siz: `watch Ø TV` ama `turn off the TV` (cihaz).

- **Kural 12:** Radyo hep the ile: `listen to the radio`, `on the radio.`

## Forms

| Form | Use | Examples |
|------|-----|----------|
| a / an | first mention, one of many | I saw **a** dog. / She ate **an** orange. |
| a / an | jobs, "one" | He is **a** teacher. / Wait **a** minute. |
| a / an | a + consonant sound, an + vowel sound | **a** university, **a** car / **an** umbrella, **an** hour |
| the | already known / mentioned before | I bought **a** car. **The** car is red. |
| the | unique things, superlatives | **The** sun is hot. / **The** best film. |
| the | places, instruments | **the** cinema, **the** hospital / play **the** guitar |
| zero (−) | plural/general, abstract | **Ø** Dogs are friendly. / I like **Ø** music. |
| zero (−) | meals, languages, sports | I had **Ø** breakfast. / She speaks **Ø** English. |
| zero (−) | names, cities, countries (mostly) | **Ø** Ali lives in **Ø** Ankara. |
| zero (−) | the + nationality adjective = people | **the** Turkish = Türkler (genel grup) |

- **Not 1:** the Turkish (halk) vs Turkish (dil): `She speaks Turkish.` ama `The Turkish drink tea.`

## 3 classic traps

Bu bölümdeki 3 tuzağa tek bakışta dikkat et.

- **İlk cümlede the yok:** okur daha bilmiyorsa a/an — ✗ I bought the car. (ilk kez anlatırken) → ✓ I bought **a** car — İkinci cümlede the serbest: The car is fast; Dinleyici "hangi araba," diye soruyorsa the erkendir.

- **Mesleklerde a şart:** TR'de eksiz, EN'de a var — ✗ She is teacher. → ✓ She is **a** teacher; ✗ He is engineer. → ✓ He is **an** engineer — Çoğulda a düşer: They are teachers.

- **Genel çoğulda the yok:** genel konuşurken at — ✗ The cats are cute animals. (genel) → ✓ **Ø Cats** are cute animals — Ama belirli kediler: The cats in our street are cute; Kural: genel = Ø, belirli = the.

## 10 example sentences

Her cümleyi sesli oku, önce İngilizceyi sonra Türkçesini söyle.

- **I have a cat and a dog.** — Bir kedim ve bir köpeğim var.

- **She ate an apple.** — Bir elma yedi.

- **The cat is sleeping.** — (Bahsettiğimiz) kedi uyuyor.

- **He is an engineer.** — O bir mühendis.

- **The moon is very bright tonight.** — Ay bu gece çok parlak.

- **I like Ø coffee.** — Kahve severim. (genel).

- **Ø Children need love.** — Çocukların sevgiye ihtiyacı vardır. (genel).

- **Let's meet at the station at 5.** — Saat 5'te istasyonda buluşalım.

- **She plays Ø tennis every day.** — Her gün tenis oynar.

- **I had Ø lunch with a friend.** — Bir arkadaşımla öğle yemeği yedim.

## Quick check

1. Fill in: ___ sun rises in the east. (a / the / Ø)
<details><summary>Answer</summary><b>The</b> sun rises in the east. (tek, herkesçe bilinen)</details>

2. Fill in: My father is ___ doctor. (a / the / Ø)
<details><summary>Answer</summary>My father is <b>a</b> doctor. (meslek → a)</details>

3. Correct: The books are expensive. (genel anlam, tüm kitaplar)
<details><summary>Answer</summary><b>Ø Books</b> are expensive. (genel çoğul → sıfır artikel)</details>
