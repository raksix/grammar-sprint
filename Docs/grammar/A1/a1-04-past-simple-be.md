# A1-04 Past Simple of Be (was / were)

## Goal
You can talk about the past with was / were, including where you were.

## Rule in 60 seconds
be'nin geçmişi: I/he/she/it → **was**, you/we/they → **were**.
Olumsuz: wasn't / weren't. Soru: Was / Were başa gelir.
was/were kendi başına soru ve olumsuz yapar, did almaz.
Zaman işaretleri: yesterday, last night, in 2010, two days ago.
there was / there were = vardı (there is/are'nin geçmişi).
Tekil isimle was, çoğulla were: There was a cat. / There were cats.
Kısa cevap: `Yes, I was.` / `No, they weren't.`
Born kalıbı was/were ile: `I was born in 2000.`
Hava durumu geçmişte: `It was cold yesterday.`
Yaş geçmişte: `I was ten in 2010.` (am değil was).
Duygu geçmişte: `They were sad after the news.`

## Forms

| Form | Pattern | Examples |
|------|---------|----------|
| + Positive | I/he/she/it + was | I **was** ill. / She **was** at home. |
| + Positive | you/we/they + were | They **were** happy. / We **were** late. |
| + Positive | there was/were | There **was** a cat. / There **were** many people. |
| − Negative | S + wasn't/weren't | He **wasn't** ready. / You **weren't** there. |
| − Negative | S + wasn't/weren't | It **wasn't** cold. / They **weren't** tired. |
| ? Question | Was/Were + S ...? | **Was** she tired? / **Were** they at school? |
| ? Wh-question | Wh + was/were + S? | **Where** were you yesterday? |
| ? There | Was/Were there ...? | **Was** there a bank here? |
| Short answer + | Yes, S + was/were. | Yes, I **was**. / Yes, they **were**. |
| Short answer − | No, S + wasn't/weren't. | No, she **wasn't**. / No, we **weren't**. |

wasn't = was not, weren't = were not.
Dün anlatırken is/are kullanma, was/were kullan.

## 3 classic traps

1. **was/were seçimi** — was sadece tekil özneler + I ile.
   - ✗ They was happy. → ✓ They **were** happy.
   - ✗ We was late. → ✓ We **were** late.
   - Ezber: I was, you were, he was, we were, they were.
2. **did ile karıştırma** — was/were kendi başına soru/olumsuz yapar, did almaz.
   - ✗ Did you were tired? → ✓ **Were** you tired?
   - ✗ I didn't was there. → ✓ I **wasn't** there.
   - did sadece normal fiillerle (go, eat, see) kullanılır.
3. **is/was zaman uyumu** — dün anlatırken is kullanma.
   - ✗ Yesterday is rainy. → ✓ Yesterday **was** rainy.
   - ✗ In 2010 I am a child. → ✓ In 2010 I **was** a child.
   - Zaman ifadesi geçmişse fiil de geçmiş olmalı.

## 10 example sentences

1. I **was** at home yesterday. — Dün evdeydim.
2. She **was** very tired last night. — Dün gece çok yorgundu.
3. They **were** in Antalya last summer. — Geçen yaz Antalya'daydılar.
4. It **was** sunny yesterday. — Dün hava güneşliydi.
5. We **weren't** ready for the exam. — Sınava hazır değildik.
6. He **wasn't** at work this morning. — Bu sabah işte değildi.
7. **Was** the film good? — Film güzel miydi?
8. **Were** you late for school? — Okula geç mi kaldın?
9. There **was** a cat in the garden. — Bahçede bir kedi vardı.
10. There **were** many people at the party. — Partide çok insan vardı.

## Quick check

1. Fill in: We ___ very happy yesterday. (was / were)
<details><summary>Answer</summary>We <b>were</b> very happy yesterday.</details>

2. Make negative: She was angry.
<details><summary>Answer</summary>She <b>wasn't</b> angry. (did kullanma!)</details>

3. Make a question: They were at home.
<details><summary>Answer</summary><b>Were</b> they at home?</details>
