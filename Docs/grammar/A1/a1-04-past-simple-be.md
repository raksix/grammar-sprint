# A1-04 Past Simple of Be (was / were)

## Goal

You can talk about the past with was / were, including where you were.

## Rule in 60 seconds

- **Kural 1:** be'nin geçmişi: I/he/she/it → **was**, you/we/they → **were**.

- **Kural 2:** Olumsuz: wasn't / weren't. Soru: Was / Were başa gelir.

- **Kural 3:** was/were kendi başına soru ve olumsuz yapar, did almaz.

- **Kural 4:** Zaman işaretleri: yesterday, last night, in 2010, two days ago.

- **Kural 5:** there was / there were = vardı (there is/are'nin geçmişi).

- **Kural 6:** Tekil isimle was, çoğulla were: There was a cat. / There were cats.

- **Kural 7:** Kısa cevap: `Yes, I was.` / `No, they weren't.`

- **Kural 8:** Born kalıbı was/were ile: `I was born in 2000.`

- **Kural 9:** Hava durumu geçmişte: `It was cold yesterday.`

- **Kural 10:** Yaş geçmişte: `I was ten in 2010.` (am değil was).

- **Kural 11:** Duygu geçmişte: `They were sad after the news.`

## Forms

| Form | Pattern | Examples |
|------|---------|----------|
| + Positive | I/he/she/it + was | I **was** ill. / She **was** at home. |
| + Positive | you/we/they + were | They **were** happy. / We **were** late. |
| + Positive | there was/were | There **was** a cat. / There **were** many people. |
| − Negative | S + wasn't/weren't | He **wasn't** ready. / You **weren't** there. |
| − Negative | S + wasn't/weren't | It **wasn't** cold. / They **weren't** tired. |
| ? Question | Was/Were + S ...? | **Was** she tired? / **Were** they at school? |
| ? Wh-question | Wh + was/were + S? | **Where** were you yesterday? |
| ? There | Was/Were there ...? | **Was** there a bank here? |
| Short answer + | Yes, S + was/were. | Yes, I **was**. / Yes, they **were**. |
| Short answer − | No, S + wasn't/weren't. | No, she **wasn't**. / No, we **weren't**. |

- **Not 1:** wasn't = was not, weren't = were not.

- **Not 2:** Dün anlatırken is/are kullanma, was/were kullan.

## 3 classic traps

Bu bölümdeki 3 tuzağa tek bakışta dikkat et.

- **was/were seçimi:** was sadece tekil özneler + I ile — ✗ They was happy. → ✓ They **were** happy; ✗ We was late. → ✓ We **were** late — Ezber: I was, you were, he was, we were, they were.

- **did ile karıştırma:** was/were kendi başına soru/olumsuz yapar, did almaz — ✗ Did you were tired? → ✓ **Were** you tired; ✗ I didn't was there. → ✓ I **wasn't** there — did sadece normal fiillerle (go, eat, see) kullanılır.

- **is/was zaman uyumu:** dün anlatırken is kullanma — ✗ Yesterday is rainy. → ✓ Yesterday **was** rainy; ✗ In 2010 I am a child. → ✓ In 2010 I **was** a child — Zaman ifadesi geçmişse fiil de geçmiş olmalı.

## 10 example sentences

Her cümleyi sesli oku, önce İngilizceyi sonra Türkçesini söyle.

- **I was at home yesterday.** — Dün evdeydim.

- **She was very tired last night.** — Dün gece çok yorgundu.

- **They were in Antalya last summer.** — Geçen yaz Antalya'daydılar.

- **It was sunny yesterday.** — Dün hava güneşliydi.

- **We weren't ready for the exam.** — Sınava hazır değildik.

- **He wasn't at work this morning.** — Bu sabah işte değildi.

- **Was the film good?** — Film güzel miydi?

- **Were you late for school?** — Okula geç mi kaldın?

- **There was a cat in the garden.** — Bahçede bir kedi vardı.

- **There were many people at the party.** — Partide çok insan vardı.

## Quick check

1. Fill in: We ___ very happy yesterday. (was / were)
<details><summary>Answer</summary>We <b>were</b> very happy yesterday.</details>

2. Make negative: She was angry.
<details><summary>Answer</summary>She <b>wasn't</b> angry. (did kullanma!)</details>

3. Make a question: They were at home.
<details><summary>Answer</summary><b>Were</b> they at home?</details>
