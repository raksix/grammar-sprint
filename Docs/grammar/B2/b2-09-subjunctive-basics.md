# B2-09 Subjunctive Basics

## Goal

Use formal demand, suggestion and urgency patterns with base verbs.

After this lesson you can:

- Write formal requests with base verbs.
- Use urgency adjectives (vital, essential) correctly.
- Complain politely with it's time + past.

## Rule in 60 seconds

Resmi taleplerde fiil yalın kalır.

I suggest (that) he go. = Gitmesini öneririm.

It's important that she be here. = Burada olması önemli.

-s takısı yok, to yok!

It's time + past ise sitem bildirir:

- It's time you left. = Gitme vaktin geldi (hâlâ gitmedin).

Kısa mantık: (should) gizlidir, fiil yalın kalır.

## Forms

| Form | Pattern | Example |
|---|---|---|
| suggest/demand | verb + that + S + V (base) | I suggest he apply now. |
| suggest/demand | verb + that + S + V (base) | They demanded she leave. |
| insist/require | insist + that + S + V | We insist he be present. |
| insist/require | require + that + S + V | The rule requires it be done. |
| important | It is + adj + that + S + V | It's vital she attend. |
| important | It is + adj + that + S + V | It's essential he rest. |
| it's time | It's (high/about) time + past | It's time you grew up. |
| it's time | It's (high/about) time + past | It's high time we left. |

Kısa Türkçe not: (should) gizlidir: "he (should) go".

Kısa Türkçe not: should düşer, fiil yalın kalır.

Not: That düşebilir: "I suggest he go" doğrudur.

## 3 classic traps

1. TR: "I suggest he goes"
   DOĞRU: "I suggest (that) he go."
   Sebep: -s takısı yok, fiil yalın.
2. TR: That'ı zorunlu sanmak
   "I suggest he go" doğrudur.
   Sebep: That opsiyoneldir.
3. TR: It's time + infinitive karıştırmak
   "It's time to leave" = genel öneri.
   Sitem için past: "It's time you left."

## 10 example sentences

1. I suggest he see a doctor.
   Bir doktora görünmesini öneririm.
2. They demanded the price be lowered.
   Fiyatın indirilmesini talep ettiler.
3. It's important she be on time.
   Zamanında olması önemli.
4. The boss insisted everyone attend.
   Patron herkesin katılmasında ısrar etti.
5. It's time you took responsibility.
   Artık sorumluluk alma vaktin geldi.
6. I propose we meet at noon.
   Öğlen buluşmamızı öneririm.
7. It's essential he rest today.
   Bugün dinlenmesi şart.
8. She requested he be informed.
   Bilgilendirilmesini rica etti.
9. It's high time they paid the rent.
   Kirayı ödeme vakti çoktan geldi.
10. Doctors recommend she stay home.
    Doktorlar evde kalmasını öneriyor.

## Quick check

1. I suggest she ___ (go) home now.
   Cevap: go. (Base verb, -s yok.)
2. It's vital he ___ (be) there.
   Cevap: be. (Urgency + base verb.)
3. "They demanded he leaves" cümlesini düzelt.
   Cevap: They demanded (that) he leave.
