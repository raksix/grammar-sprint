# B2-11 Advanced Conjunctions and Linkers

## Goal

Contrast and concede ideas with linkers like whereas, nevertheless and despite.

- **Karşıtlık:** Contrast two facts with whereas/while.
- **Ödün:** Concede with however/nevertheless.
- **İsim:** Use despite + noun/-ing without errors.
- **TR özet:** Cümle varsa although, isim varsa despite.

## Rule in 60 seconds

Cümle + cümle bağlanır; isim öncesi kalıp değişir.

- **Karşıtlık:** whereas/while ile — "I like tea, whereas he likes coffee."
- **Ödün:** however/nevertheless ile — nokta + virgül gerekir.
- **İsim:** despite/in spite of + noun/-ing.
- **Cümle:** Although + cümle alır.
- **Yasak:** despite asla + cümle almaz.
- **Mantık:** Cümle varsa although, isim varsa despite.
- **TR ipucu:** "Despite it rained" olmaz; "Although it rained" olur.

## Forms

Patterns at a glance.

| Linker | Pattern | Example |
|---|---|---|
| whereas/while | clause, whereas + clause | I like tea, whereas he likes coffee. |
| whereas/while | clause, whereas + clause | She is calm, while her brother panics. |
| however | clause. However, + clause | It rained. However, we stayed. |
| however | clause. However, + clause | He is rich. However, he isn't happy. |
| nevertheless | clause; nevertheless, + clause | It was late; nevertheless, we left. |
| nevertheless | clause; nevertheless, + clause | She was ill; nevertheless, she worked. |
| despite | despite + noun/-ing | Despite the rain, we walked. |
| despite | despite + noun/-ing | Despite being tired, he ran. |

- **TR not:** Despite + cümle YANLIŞ.
- **TR not:** "Despite it rained" olmaz; "Although it rained" olur.
- **Kural:** In spite of + isim ya da -ing ister: "being tired".

## 3 classic traps

En sık yapılan 3 hata.

- **Trap 1 — despite + cümle:** ❌ "Despite it was raining" → ✅ "Although it was raining" / "Despite the rain."
- **Neden:** Despite cümle almaz.
- **Trap 2 — eksik -ing:** ❌ "In spite of tired" → ✅ "In spite of being tired."
- **Neden:** İsim ya da -ing gerekir.
- **Trap 3 — however bağlaç gibi:** ❌ "He came, however he left" → ✅ "He came. However, he left early."
- **Neden:** However cümle başına nokta + virgül ister.

## 10 example sentences

Read aloud and pause at each linker.

- **I prefer the bus, whereas she prefers the metro.** — Ben otobüsü tercih ederim, o ise metroyu.
- **He saved money, while his sister spent it all.** — O para biriktirdi, kız kardeşi ise hepsini harcadı.
- **It was freezing. However, the match continued.** — Dondurucu soğuktu. Yine de maç sürdü.
- **Despite the traffic, we arrived on time.** — Trafiğe rağmen zamanında vardık.
- **In spite of feeling sick, she performed.** — Hasta hissetmesine rağmen sahneye çıktı.
- **Although he is young, he is very wise.** — Genç olmasına rağmen çok bilge.
- **The hotel was costly; nevertheless, it was full.** — Otel pahalıydı; yine de doluydu.
- **Whereas Istanbul is crowded, Sinop is calm.** — İstanbul kalabalıkken Sinop sakindir.
- **Despite having little money, they travel a lot.** — Az paraları olmasına rağmen çok gezerler.
- **Even though it rained, we enjoyed the picnic.** — Yağmur yağmasına rağmen piknikten keyif aldık.

## Quick check

Boşluğu doldur, sonra cevapla karşılaştır.

- **1. ___ the storm, the ferry sailed.**
- **Cevap 1:** Despite / In spite of — + noun.
- **2. She is shy, ___ her sister is outgoing.**
- **Cevap 2:** whereas — contrast.
- **3. "Despite she was ill" cümlesini düzelt.**
- **Cevap 3:** Although she was ill / Despite her illness.
