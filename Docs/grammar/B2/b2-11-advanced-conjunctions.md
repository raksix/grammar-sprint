# B2-11 Advanced Conjunctions and Linkers

## Goal

Contrast and concede ideas with linkers like whereas, nevertheless and despite.

After this lesson you can:

- Contrast two facts with whereas/while.
- Concede with however/nevertheless.
- Use despite + noun/-ing without errors.

## Rule in 60 seconds

Cümle + cümle bağlanır.

whereas/while ile (I like tea, whereas he likes coffee).

however/nevertheless ile (nokta + virgül gerekir).

İsim/-ing öncesi farklıdır:

- despite/in spite of + noun/-ing.

Although + cümle alır.

despite asla + cümle almaz.

Kısa mantık: cümle varsa although, isim varsa despite.

## Forms

| Linker | Pattern | Example |
|---|---|---|
| whereas/while | clause, whereas + clause | I like tea, whereas he likes coffee. |
| whereas/while | clause, whereas + clause | She is calm, while her brother panics. |
| however | clause. However, + clause | It rained. However, we stayed. |
| however | clause. However, + clause | He is rich. However, he isn't happy. |
| nevertheless | clause; nevertheless, + clause | It was late; nevertheless, we left. |
| nevertheless | clause; nevertheless, + clause | She was ill; nevertheless, she worked. |
| despite | despite + noun/-ing | Despite the rain, we walked. |
| despite | despite + noun/-ing | Despite being tired, he ran. |

Kısa Türkçe not: Despite + cümle YANLIŞ.

Kısa Türkçe not: "Despite it rained" olmaz; "Although it rained" olur.

Not: In spite of + isim ya da -ing ister: "being tired".

## 3 classic traps

1. TR: "Despite it was raining"
   DOĞRU: "Although it was raining" / "Despite the rain."
   Sebep: Despite cümle almaz.
2. TR: "In spite of tired"
   DOĞRU: "In spite of being tired."
   Sebep: İsim ya da -ing gerekir.
3. TR: However'a bağlaç muamelesi
   "He came, however he left" YANLIŞ.
   DOĞRU: "He came. However, he left early."

## 10 example sentences

1. I prefer the bus, whereas she prefers the metro.
   Ben otobüsü tercih ederim, o ise metroyu.
2. He saved money, while his sister spent it all.
   O para biriktirdi, kız kardeşi ise hepsini harcadı.
3. It was freezing. However, the match continued.
   Dondurucu soğuktu. Yine de maç sürdü.
4. Despite the traffic, we arrived on time.
   Trafiğe rağmen zamanında vardık.
5. In spite of feeling sick, she performed.
   Hasta hissetmesine rağmen sahneye çıktı.
6. Although he is young, he is very wise.
   Genç olmasına rağmen çok bilge.
7. The hotel was costly; nevertheless, it was full.
   Otel pahalıydı; yine de doluydu.
8. Whereas Istanbul is crowded, Sinop is calm.
   İstanbul kalabalıkken Sinop sakindir.
9. Despite having little money, they travel a lot.
   Az paraları olmasına rağmen çok gezerler.
10. Even though it rained, we enjoyed the picnic.
    Yağmur yağmasına rağmen piknikten keyif aldık.

## Quick check

1. ___ the storm, the ferry sailed.
   Cevap: Despite / In spite of. (+ noun.)
2. She is shy, ___ her sister is outgoing.
   Cevap: whereas. (Contrast.)
3. "Despite she was ill" cümlesini düzelt.
   Cevap: Although she was ill / Despite her illness.
