# B1-11 Gerund vs Infinitive

## Goal

- Choose verb + -ing or verb + to after key verbs.
- Master meaning-changers: remember/stop/try/forget/regret.
- TR: Hangi fiilden sonra -ing, hangisinden sonra to gelir; anlam degisenleri ogrenmek.

## Rule in 60 seconds

- Some verbs take -ing (enjoy, mind, suggest, avoid), some take to (decide, plan, want, agree).
- A few take both with meaning change: remember, forget, stop, try, regret.
- TR: Liste ezberle + anlam degisen 5 fiile dikkat.

## Forms

| Pattern | Verbs | Example |
|---|---|---|
| verb + -ing | enjoy, mind, avoid, suggest, finish, keep, practise | I enjoy swimming. |
| verb + to | decide, plan, want, need, agree, hope, learn | She decided to leave. |
| both, same meaning | start, begin, continue, like, love, hate | It started to rain / raining. |
| both, meaning change | remember, forget, stop, try, regret | see below |

- Meaning changers:
  - remember doing = past memory; remember to do = don't forget a task.
  - stop doing = quit; stop to do = pause in order to.
  - try doing = experiment; try to do = attempt something hard.
  - TR: "Sigarayi birakmak" = stop smoking; "mola verip sigara icmek" = stop to smoke.
- After prepositions always -ing: "I am good at cooking." "Thank you for coming."
- TR: Edattan sonra hep -ing gelir.

```text
enjoy + DOING / decide + TO DO
I REMEMBER LOCKING the door. (hatirliyorum — gecmis)
REMEMBER TO LOCK the door! (unutma — gorev)
We STOPPED TO EAT. (yemek icin durduk) / We STOPPED EATING junk food. (biraktik)
```

## 3 classic traps

### 1. to after -ing verbs

- Wrong: I enjoy to swim.
- Right: I enjoy swimming.
- TR: enjoy, mind, avoid, suggest + -ing.

### 2. -ing after to-verbs

- Wrong: She decided leaving.
- Right: She decided to leave.
- TR: decide, plan, want, agree + to.

### 3. Swapping the meaning-changers

- Wrong meaning: "I stopped to smoke." (= I paused to have a cigarette)
- Intended: "I stopped smoking." (= I quit)
- TR: Anlam tamamen degisir; cumleyi iki kez kontrol et.

## 10 example sentences

1. I enjoy reading detective novels. — Polisiye roman okumaktan keyif alirim.
2. She decided to study medicine. — Tip okumaya karar verdi.
3. Would you mind opening the window? — Pencereyi acar misin? (rahatsiz olur musun)
4. I remember meeting him at a wedding. — Onunla bir dugunde tanistigimi hatirliyorum.
5. Remember to buy some bread! — Ekmek almayi unutma!
6. He stopped smoking last year. — Gecen yil sigarayi birakti.
7. We stopped to buy some water. — Su almak icin durduk.
8. Try restarting your phone. — Telefonunu yeniden baslatmayi dene. (yontem onerisi)
9. She avoided answering the question. — Soruyu cevaplamaktan kacindi.
10. I regret telling him the secret. — Ona sirri soyledigime pismanim.

## Quick check

1. I enjoy ___ (read). → reading.
2. She decided ___ (leave). → to leave.
3. Past memory: I remember ___ (lock) it. → locking.
4. Don't forget: Remember ___ (call) her! → to call.
5. Quit: He stopped ___. (smoke) → smoking.
6. Pause to: We stopped ___ (drink) tea. → to drink.
7. After preposition: good at ___ (cook). → cooking.
8. Fix: "Would you mind to help?" → Would you mind helping?
