# B1-11 Gerund vs Infinitive

## Goal

- **Not:** Choose verb + -ing or verb + to after key verbs.
- **Master meaning-changers:** remember/stop/try/forget/regret.
- **TR:** Hangi fiilden sonra -ing, hangisinden sonra to gelir; anlam degisenleri ogrenmek.


## Rule in 60 seconds

- **Not:** Some verbs take -ing (enjoy, mind, suggest, avoid), some take to (decide, plan, want, agree).
- **A few take both with meaning change:** remember, forget, stop, try, regret.
- **TR:** Liste ezberle + anlam degisen 5 fiile dikkat.


## Forms

| Pattern | Verbs | Example |
|---|---|---|
| verb + -ing | enjoy, mind, avoid, suggest, finish, keep, practise | I enjoy swimming. |
| verb + to | decide, plan, want, need, agree, hope, learn | She decided to leave. |
| both, same meaning | start, begin, continue, like, love, hate | It started to rain / raining. |
| both, meaning change | remember, forget, stop, try, regret | see below |

- **Not:** Meaning changers:
- **Not:** remember doing = past memory; remember to do = don't forget a task.
- **Not:** stop doing = quit; stop to do = pause in order to.
- **Not:** try doing = experiment; try to do = attempt something hard.
- **TR:** "Sigarayi birakmak" = stop smoking; "mola verip sigara icmek" = stop to smoke.
- **After prepositions always -ing:** "I am good at cooking." "Thank you for coming."
- **TR:** Edattan sonra hep -ing gelir.

- **Formül:** `enjoy + DOING / decide + TO DO`
- **Örnek:** `I REMEMBER LOCKING the door. (hatirliyorum — gecmis)`
- **Örnek:** `REMEMBER TO LOCK the door! (unutma — gorev)`
- **Örnek:** `We STOPPED TO EAT. (yemek icin durduk) / We STOPPED EATING junk food. (biraktik)`


## 3 classic traps

- **Tuzak 1 — to after -ing verbs:** ❌ `I enjoy to swim.` → ✅ `I enjoy swimming.`
- **TR:** enjoy, mind, avoid, suggest + -ing.

- **Tuzak 2 — -ing after to-verbs:** ❌ `She decided leaving.` → ✅ `She decided to leave.`
- **TR:** decide, plan, want, agree + to.

- **Tuzak 3 — Swapping the meaning-changers:**
- **Wrong meaning:** "I stopped to smoke." (= I paused to have a cigarette)
- **Intended:** "I stopped smoking." (= I quit)
- **TR:** Anlam tamamen degisir; cumleyi iki kez kontrol et.


## 10 example sentences

- **I enjoy reading detective novels.** — Polisiye roman okumaktan keyif alirim.
- **She decided to study medicine.** — Tip okumaya karar verdi.
- **Would you mind opening the window?.** — Pencereyi acar misin? (rahatsiz olur musun)
- **I remember meeting him at a wedding.** — Onunla bir dugunde tanistigimi hatirliyorum.
- **Remember to buy some bread!.** — Ekmek almayi unutma!
- **He stopped smoking last year.** — Gecen yil sigarayi birakti.
- **We stopped to buy some water.** — Su almak icin durduk.
- **Try restarting your phone.** — Telefonunu yeniden baslatmayi dene. (yontem onerisi)
- **She avoided answering the question.** — Soruyu cevaplamaktan kacindi.
- **I regret telling him the secret.** — Ona sirri soyledigime pismanim.


## Quick check

- **Soru 1:** I enjoy ___ (read). → reading.
- **Soru 2:** She decided ___ (leave). → to leave.
- **Soru 3:** Past memory: I remember ___ (lock) it. → locking.
- **Soru 4:** Don't forget: Remember ___ (call) her! → to call.
- **Soru 5:** Quit: He stopped ___. (smoke) → smoking.
- **Soru 6:** Pause to: We stopped ___ (drink) tea. → to drink.
- **Soru 7:** After preposition: good at ___ (cook). → cooking.
- **Soru 8:** Fix: "Would you mind to help?" → Would you mind helping?
