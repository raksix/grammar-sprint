# B1-01 Present Perfect

## Goal

- **Not:** Use have/has + past participle (V3) to talk about past actions with present results.
- **Not:** Choose correctly between ever/never/just/already/yet and for/since.
- **TR:** Gecmiste olup sonucu bugunu etkileyen olaylari anlatmak.


## Rule in 60 seconds

- **Form:** **have/has + V3** (I have seen, she has gone).
- **Use it for:** life experience, recent news with present result, unfinished time (today, this week).
- **TR:** Ne zaman oldugu degil, sonuc onemliyse present perfect kullan.


## Forms

| Subject | Affirmative | Negative | Question |
|---|---|---|---|
| I / you / we / they | I have finished | I have not (haven't) finished | Have you finished? |
| he / she / it | She has finished | She has not (hasn't) finished | Has she finished? |

```pattern
have/has + V3
```

- **Past participle:** regular + -ed (worked), irregular must be memorised (gone, seen, eaten, written).
- **TR:** Duzensiz fiillerin 3. halini ezberle: go-went-gone, see-saw-seen.
- **Not:** Time markers:
- **`ever` (questions:** Have you ever...?), `never` (I have never...).
- **Not:** `just` (a moment ago), `already` (sooner than expected), `yet` (questions + negatives, end of sentence).
- **Not:** `for` + duration (for 3 years), `since` + starting point (since 2020).
- **TR:** for + sure, since + baslangic noktasi.

- **Örnek:** `have/has + V3`
- **Örnek:** `Have you EVER eaten sushi? — No, I have NEVER tried it.`
- **Örnek:** `I have JUST finished. / I have ALREADY seen it. / I haven't called YET.`
- **Örnek:** `She has lived here FOR 5 years / SINCE 2020.`


## 3 classic traps

- **Tuzak 1 — Present perfect with a finished past time word:** ❌ `I have seen him yesterday.` → ✅ `I saw him yesterday.`
- **TR:** yesterday, last night, in 2019 gibi bitmis zaman varsa past simple kullan.

- **Tuzak 2 — For vs since swap:** ❌ `I have worked here since 5 years.` → ✅ `I have worked here for 5 years / since 2020.`
- **TR:** for + sure (5 years), since + tarih/an (2020, Monday, I was a child).

- **Tuzak 3 — Gone vs been:**
- **Not:** `gone` = went and is still there. `been` = went and came back.
- **Not:** He has gone to Ankara (he is there now). He has been to Ankara (he visited before).
- **TR:** gone = orada, been = gidip donmus.


## 10 example sentences

- **I have just finished my homework.** — Odevimi az once bitirdim.
- **She has already seen that film twice.** — O filmi iki kez izledi bile.
- **Have you ever tried Turkish coffee?.** — Hic Turk kahvesi denedin mi?
- **I have never been to Izmir.** — Hic Izmir'e gitmedim.
- **They haven't called yet.** — Henuz aramadilar.
- **We have lived in Bursa since 2018.** — 2018'den beri Bursa'da yasiyoruz.
- **He has worked here for ten years.** — On yildir burada calisiyor.
- **The bus hasn't arrived yet.** — Otobus henuz gelmedi.
- **She has just lost her keys.** — Anahtarlarini az once kaybetti. (sonuc: su an yok)
- **Have you finished your project yet?.** — Projeni bitirdin mi henuz?


## Quick check

- **Soru 1:** I ___ (never / see) snow. → I have never seen snow.
- **Soru 2:** She ___ (work) here ___ 2021. → She has worked here since 2021.
- **Soru 3:** They ___ (just / arrive). → They have just arrived.
- **Soru 4:** ___ you ___ (eat) yet? → Have you eaten yet?
- **Soru 5:** I saw him yesterday. (Why not present perfect? → finished time word.)
- **Soru 6:** gone or been? "He has ___ to London, he is there now." → gone.
- **Soru 7:** Fix: "I have done it last week." → I did it last week.
- **Soru 8:** He has lived here ___ five years. (for/since) → for.
