# B1-12 Question Tags and Linkers

## Goal

- **Not:** Build correct question tags (isn't it?, didn't you?) with matching polarity.
- **Not:** Link ideas with although/despite/however/because of/in spite of.
- **TR:** Onay isteyen kisa sorular ve baglaclarla fikirleri baglamak.


## Rule in 60 seconds

- **Not:** Tag = opposite polarity + same auxiliary + same subject pronoun.
- **Not:** Positive statement → negative tag; negative statement → positive tag.
- **TR:** Cumle olumluysa tag olumsuz, olumsuzsa olumlu.


## Forms

| Statement | Tag | Note |
|---|---|---|
| You are Turkish, | aren't you? | be → be |
| She works here, | doesn't she? | present simple → do |
| They came late, | didn't they? | past simple → did |
| We haven't met, | have we? | negative → positive |
| You can swim, | can't you? | modal repeats |
| Let's go, | shall we? | let's = shall we (fixed) |
| I'm late, | aren't I? | exception: aren't I |

- **Not:** Linkers:
- **Contrast:** although/though/even though + clause; despite/in spite of + noun/-ing; however + comma.
- **Cause:** because + clause; because of + noun/-ing; so + result.
- **TR:** although + cumle, despite + isim/-ing.

- **Örnek:** `TAG: You LIKE tea, DON'T you? / He DIDN'T call, DID he?`
- **Örnek:** `CONTRAST: ALTHOUGH it was raining, we went out. = DESPITE the rain, we went out.`
- **Örnek:** `CAUSE: BECAUSE it rained, we stayed. = BECAUSE OF the rain, we stayed.`
- **Örnek:** `HOWEVER, we stayed home. (virgule dikkat)`


## 3 classic traps

- **Tuzak 1 — Same-polarity tags:** ❌ `You are coming, are you?` → ✅ `You are coming, aren't you?`
- **TR:** Tag zit kutuplu olur (olumlu-olumsuz).

- **Tuzak 2 — Despite + full clause:** ❌ `Despite it was raining, we went out.` → ✅ `Despite the rain, we went out. / Although it was raining, we went out.`
- **TR:** despite/in spite of/because of + isim ya da -ing.

- **Tuzak 3 — Because vs because of:** ❌ `We stayed home because of it was raining.` → ✅ `We stayed home because it was raining. / ...because of the rain.`
- **TR:** because + cumle, because of + isim.


## 10 example sentences

- **You're from Ankara, aren't you?.** — Ankaralisin, degil mi?
- **She can't drive, can she?.** — Araba kullanamaz, degil mi?
- **They arrived late, didn't they?.** — Gec geldiler, degil mi?
- **You haven't seen my keys, have you?.** — Anahtarlarimi gormedin, degil mi?
- **Let's take a break, shall we?.** — Ara verelim, olur mu?
- **Although it was expensive, we bought it.** — Pahali olmasina ragmen aldik.
- **Despite the rain, the match continued.** — Yagmura ragmen mac devam etti.
- **He was tired; however, he kept working.** — Yorgundu; yine de calismaya devam etti.
- **The flight was cancelled because of the storm.** — Firtina yuzunden ucus iptal edildi.
- **In spite of being sick, she came to class.** — Hasta olmasina ragmen derse geldi.


## Quick check

- **Soru 1:** You speak English, ___ you? → don't.
- **Soru 2:** She didn't call, ___ she? → did.
- **Soru 3:** Fix: "He is late, is he?" → He is late, isn't he?
- **Soru 4:** ___ the rain, we stayed. (Despite/Although + noun) → Despite.
- **Soru 5:** ___ it rained, we stayed. → Because / Although (clause).
- **Soru 6:** Fix: "Despite it was late, we left." → Despite the lateness... / Although it was late...
- **Soru 7:** Let's go, ___ we? → shall.
- **Soru 8:** I'm right, ___ I? → aren't.
