# B1-12 Question Tags and Linkers

## Goal

- Build correct question tags (isn't it?, didn't you?) with matching polarity.
- Link ideas with although/despite/however/because of/in spite of.
- TR: Onay isteyen kisa sorular ve baglaclarla fikirleri baglamak.

## Rule in 60 seconds

- Tag = opposite polarity + same auxiliary + same subject pronoun.
- Positive statement → negative tag; negative statement → positive tag.
- TR: Cumle olumluysa tag olumsuz, olumsuzsa olumlu.

## Forms

| Statement | Tag | Note |
|---|---|---|
| You are Turkish, | aren't you? | be → be |
| She works here, | doesn't she? | present simple → do |
| They came late, | didn't they? | past simple → did |
| We haven't met, | have we? | negative → positive |
| You can swim, | can't you? | modal repeats |
| Let's go, | shall we? | let's = shall we (fixed) |
| I'm late, | aren't I? | exception: aren't I |

- Linkers:
  - Contrast: although/though/even though + clause; despite/in spite of + noun/-ing; however + comma.
  - Cause: because + clause; because of + noun/-ing; so + result.
  - TR: although + cumle, despite + isim/-ing.

```text
TAG: You LIKE tea, DON'T you? / He DIDN'T call, DID he?
CONTRAST: ALTHOUGH it was raining, we went out. = DESPITE the rain, we went out.
CAUSE: BECAUSE it rained, we stayed. = BECAUSE OF the rain, we stayed.
HOWEVER, we stayed home. (virgule dikkat)
```

## 3 classic traps

### 1. Same-polarity tags

- Wrong: You are coming, are you?
- Right: You are coming, aren't you?
- TR: Tag zit kutuplu olur (olumlu-olumsuz).

### 2. Despite + full clause

- Wrong: Despite it was raining, we went out.
- Right: Despite the rain, we went out. / Although it was raining, we went out.
- TR: despite/in spite of/because of + isim ya da -ing.

### 3. Because vs because of

- Wrong: We stayed home because of it was raining.
- Right: We stayed home because it was raining. / ...because of the rain.
- TR: because + cumle, because of + isim.

## 10 example sentences

1. You're from Ankara, aren't you? — Ankaralisin, degil mi?
2. She can't drive, can she? — Araba kullanamaz, degil mi?
3. They arrived late, didn't they? — Gec geldiler, degil mi?
4. You haven't seen my keys, have you? — Anahtarlarimi gormedin, degil mi?
5. Let's take a break, shall we? — Ara verelim, olur mu?
6. Although it was expensive, we bought it. — Pahali olmasina ragmen aldik.
7. Despite the rain, the match continued. — Yagmura ragmen mac devam etti.
8. He was tired; however, he kept working. — Yorgundu; yine de calismaya devam etti.
9. The flight was cancelled because of the storm. — Firtina yuzunden ucus iptal edildi.
10. In spite of being sick, she came to class. — Hasta olmasina ragmen derse geldi.

## Quick check

1. You speak English, ___ you? → don't.
2. She didn't call, ___ she? → did.
3. Fix: "He is late, is he?" → He is late, isn't he?
4. ___ the rain, we stayed. (Despite/Although + noun) → Despite.
5. ___ it rained, we stayed. → Because / Although (clause).
6. Fix: "Despite it was late, we left." → Despite the lateness... / Although it was late...
7. Let's go, ___ we? → shall.
8. I'm right, ___ I? → aren't.
