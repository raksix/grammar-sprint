# B1-07 Reported Speech

## Goal

- Report statements, questions, and commands with correct backshift.
- Choose say vs tell correctly and shift time expressions.
- TR: Baskasinin sozunu aktarmak (dedi ki, sordu, emretti).

## Rule in 60 seconds

- Reporting verb in past → shift tenses one step back (present → past, past → past perfect).
- Questions lose question word order; commands become to-infinitive.
- TR: Aktarma fiili past ise zaman bir adim geriye kayar.

## Forms

| Direct | Reported |
|---|---|
| "I am tired." | He said (that) he was tired. |
| "We saw it." | They said they had seen it. |
| "I will call." | She said she would call. |
| "Can you help?" | He asked me to help. / He asked if I could help. |
| "Open the door." | She told me to open the door. |
| "Don't be late." | He told us not to be late. |

- `say` + (no person): He said he was busy. `tell` + person: He told me he was busy.
- TR: tell'den sonra mutlaka kisi gelir (told me/him/us).
- Yes/no questions: asked if/whether + statement order ("He asked if I was ready").
- Wh-questions keep the wh-word: "She asked where I lived."
- Time shifts: today → that day, yesterday → the day before, tomorrow → the next day, now → then.

```text
STATEMENT: "I LIVE here." → He said he LIVED there.
QUESTION: "WHERE do you LIVE?" → She asked where I LIVED.
COMMAND: "SIT down." → He told me TO SIT down.
```

## 3 classic traps

### 1. Keeping question word order in reported questions

- Wrong: She asked where did I live.
- Right: She asked where I lived.
- TR: Aktarilan soruda duz cumle dizilimi kullan.

### 2. Tell without a person

- Wrong: He told that he was ill.
- Right: He said that he was ill. / He told me that he was ill.
- TR: told + kisi, said + kisi yok.

### 3. Forgetting backshift after past reporting verb

- Wrong: She said she is ready. (she spoke yesterday)
- Right: She said she was ready.
- TR: Aktarma gecmiste ise zamani geriye kaydir.

## 10 example sentences

1. He said he was tired. — Yorgun oldugunu soyledi.
2. She told me she would call later. — Bana sonra arayacagini soyledi.
3. They said they had finished the project. — Projeyi bitirdiklerini soylediler.
4. She asked me where I lived. — Nerede yasadigimi sordu.
5. He asked if I needed help. — Yardima ihtiyacim olup olmadigini sordu.
6. The teacher told us to open our books. — Ogretmen kitaplarimizi acmamizi soyledi.
7. She told him not to be late. — Gec kalmamasini soyledi.
8. He said he had seen the film before. — Filmi daha once izledigini soyledi.
9. They asked what time the train left. — Trenin saat kacta kalktigini sordular.
10. My mum said I had to tidy my room. — Annem odami toplamam gerektigini soyledi.

## Quick check

1. "I am busy." → He said he ___ busy. → was.
2. Fix: "He told that he was ill." → He said... / He told me...
3. "Where do you live?" → She asked where I ___. → lived.
4. "Close the door." → He told me ___ close the door. → to.
5. "Don't shout." → She told us ___ shout. → not to.
6. "Are you ready?" → He asked ___ I was ready. → if.
7. "I saw it yesterday." → She said she had seen it ___ . → the day before.
8. say or tell? "She ___ me the truth." → told.
