# B1-07 Reported Speech

## Goal

- **Not:** Report statements, questions, and commands with correct backshift.
- **Not:** Choose say vs tell correctly and shift time expressions.
- **TR:** Baskasinin sozunu aktarmak (dedi ki, sordu, emretti).


## Rule in 60 seconds

- **Not:** Reporting verb in past → shift tenses one step back (present → past, past → past perfect).
- **Not:** Questions lose question word order; commands become to-infinitive.
- **TR:** Aktarma fiili past ise zaman bir adim geriye kayar.


## Forms

| Direct | Reported |
|---|---|
| "I am tired." | He said (that) he was tired. |
| "We saw it." | They said they had seen it. |
| "I will call." | She said she would call. |
| "Can you help?" | He asked me to help. / He asked if I could help. |
| "Open the door." | She told me to open the door. |
| "Don't be late." | He told us not to be late. |

- **`say` + (no person):** He said he was busy. `tell` + person: He told me he was busy.
- **TR:** tell'den sonra mutlaka kisi gelir (told me/him/us).
- **Yes/no questions:** asked if/whether + statement order ("He asked if I was ready").
- **Wh-questions keep the wh-word:** "She asked where I lived."
- **Time shifts:** today → that day, yesterday → the day before, tomorrow → the next day, now → then.

- **Örnek:** `STATEMENT: "I LIVE here." → He said he LIVED there.`
- **Örnek:** `QUESTION: "WHERE do you LIVE?" → She asked where I LIVED.`
- **Örnek:** `COMMAND: "SIT down." → He told me TO SIT down.`


## 3 classic traps

- **Tuzak 1 — Keeping question word order in reported questions:** ❌ `She asked where did I live.` → ✅ `She asked where I lived.`
- **TR:** Aktarilan soruda duz cumle dizilimi kullan.

- **Tuzak 2 — Tell without a person:** ❌ `He told that he was ill.` → ✅ `He said that he was ill. / He told me that he was ill.`
- **TR:** told + kisi, said + kisi yok.

- **Tuzak 3 — Forgetting backshift after past reporting verb:** ❌ `She said she is ready. (she spoke yesterday)` → ✅ `She said she was ready.`
- **TR:** Aktarma gecmiste ise zamani geriye kaydir.


## 10 example sentences

- **He said he was tired.** — Yorgun oldugunu soyledi.
- **She told me she would call later.** — Bana sonra arayacagini soyledi.
- **They said they had finished the project.** — Projeyi bitirdiklerini soylediler.
- **She asked me where I lived.** — Nerede yasadigimi sordu.
- **He asked if I needed help.** — Yardima ihtiyacim olup olmadigini sordu.
- **The teacher told us to open our books.** — Ogretmen kitaplarimizi acmamizi soyledi.
- **She told him not to be late.** — Gec kalmamasini soyledi.
- **He said he had seen the film before.** — Filmi daha once izledigini soyledi.
- **They asked what time the train left.** — Trenin saat kacta kalktigini sordular.
- **My mum said I had to tidy my room.** — Annem odami toplamam gerektigini soyledi.


## Quick check

- **Soru 1:** "I am busy." → He said he ___ busy. → was.
- **Soru 2:** Fix: "He told that he was ill." → He said... / He told me...
- **Soru 3:** "Where do you live?" → She asked where I ___. → lived.
- **Soru 4:** "Close the door." → He told me ___ close the door. → to.
- **Soru 5:** "Don't shout." → She told us ___ shout. → not to.
- **Soru 6:** "Are you ready?" → He asked ___ I was ready. → if.
- **Soru 7:** "I saw it yesterday." → She said she had seen it ___ . → the day before.
- **Soru 8:** say or tell? "She ___ me the truth." → told.
