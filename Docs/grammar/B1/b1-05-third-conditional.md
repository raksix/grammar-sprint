# B1-05 Third Conditional

## Goal

- **Not:** Talk about past regrets and imaginary past results with if + past perfect, would have + V3.
- **Not:** Contrast second (present imagination) vs third (past regret) conditionals.
- **TR:** Gecmiste olmamis seyler icin pismanlik ve hayal kurmak.


## Rule in 60 seconds

- **Form:** **If + past perfect, would/could/might have + V3**.
- **Not:** Both parts refer to the past; neither happened.
- **TR:** If'li kisimda had + V3, sonuc kisminda would have + V3.


## Forms

| If-clause | Main clause |
|---|---|
| If I had studied harder, | I would have passed. |
| If she had left earlier, | she wouldn't have missed the bus. |
| If we had known, | we could have helped. |

- **Contractions:** I'd have = I would have; hadn't = had not.
- **TR:** If I'd known = Bilseydim.
- **Modal shades:** would have (certain), could have (ability), might have (possibility).
- **Inversion (formal):** Had I known, I would have stayed. (= If I had known...)

- **Örnek:** `If + HAD + V3, would HAVE + V3`
- **Örnek:** `If you HAD studied, you WOULD HAVE passed.`
- **Örnek:** `If we HADN'T hurried, we WOULD HAVE missed the flight.`
- **Kısaltma:** I'd have studied = I would have studied.


## 3 classic traps

- **Tuzak 1 — Mixing second and third conditional:** ❌ `If I had studied, I would pass.` → ✅ `If I had studied, I would have passed.`
- **Not:** Parantez içi anlam farkı: mixed conditional bugünkü sonucu anlatır, third past sonucu.
- **TR:** Sonuc gecmiste ise would have + V3 sart.

- **Tuzak 2 — Would have in the if-clause:** ❌ `If I would have known, I would have come.` → ✅ `If I had known, I would have come.`
- **TR:** If'li kisma would gelmez, had gelir.

- **Tuzak 3 — Forgetting have in the main clause:** ❌ `I would passed the exam.` → ✅ `I would have passed the exam.`
- **TR:** would + have + V3, have dusmez.


## 10 example sentences

- **If I had studied harder, I would have passed.** — Daha cok calsaydim geceridim.
- **If she had left earlier, she wouldn't have missed the train.** — Erken ciksaydi treni kacirmazdi.
- **If we had known you were ill, we would have visited.** — Hasta oldugunu bilseydik ziyaret ederdik.
- **He wouldn't have crashed if he had driven slowly.** — Yavas surseydi kaza yapmazdi.
- **If they had saved money, they could have bought a house.** — Para biriktirselerdi ev alabilirlerdi.
- **I would have called you, but I had lost your number.** — Seni arardim ama numaranı kaybetmistim.
- **If you hadn't helped me, I would have failed.** — Bana yardim etmeseydin kalirdim.
- **She might have won if she had tried harder.** — Daha cok deneseydi kazanabilirdi.
- **If it hadn't rained, we would have gone hiking.** — Yagmur yagmasaydi yuruyuse giderdik.
- **Had I seen you, I would have said hello.** — Seni gorseydim selam verirdim.


## Quick check

- **Soru 1:** If I ___ (know), I would have helped. → had known.
- **Soru 2:** She ___ have missed the bus if she had run. (would/wouldn't) → wouldn't.
- **Soru 3:** Fix: "If I would have seen him, I'd have told him." → If I had seen him...
- **Soru 4:** If they had left earlier, they ___ (arrive) on time. → would have arrived.
- **Soru 5:** Second or third? "If I had money now, I'd buy it." → second (present).
- **Soru 6:** He could ___ helped if he had known. (have/has) → have.
- **Soru 7:** Fix: "I would passed if I tried." → I would have passed if I had tried.
- **Soru 8:** Invert: "If I had seen you..." → Had I seen you...
