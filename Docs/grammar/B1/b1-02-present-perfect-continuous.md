# B1-02 Present Perfect Continuous

## Goal

- **Not:** Use have/has been + V-ing for actions that started in the past and still continue.
- **Not:** Ask and answer "how long" questions correctly.
- **TR:** Bir suredir devam eden eylemleri anlatmak.


## Rule in 60 seconds

- **Form:** **have/has been + V-ing** (I have been waiting, she has been working).
- **Not:** Use it when the duration or the ongoing process matters.
- **TR:** Ne kadar suredir devam ediyor sorusunun cevabi bu tense.


## Forms

| Subject | Affirmative | Negative | Question |
|---|---|---|---|
| I / you / we / they | I have been waiting | I haven't been waiting | Have you been waiting? |
| he / she / it | She has been waiting | She hasn't been waiting | Has she been waiting? |

- **Core pattern:** `have/has been + verb-ing`.
- **TR:** been sart, sonra -ing gelir.
- **Typical markers:** `for` (for 2 hours), `since` (since morning), `how long`, `all day/week`.
- **Short answers:** "How long have you been learning English?" — "For three years."

- **Formül:** `have/has + been + V-ing`
- **Örnek:** `I have been studying SINCE 9 am.`
- **Örnek:** `She has been working here FOR six months.`
- **Örnek:** `HOW LONG have you been waiting?`

- **Not:** Simple vs continuous:
- **Simple (have done):** result finished — "I have painted the room."
- **Continuous (have been doing):** process ongoing — "I have been painting the room all day."
- **TR:** Bitmis sonuc mu, devam eden surec mi? Ona gore sec.


## 3 classic traps

- **Tuzak 1 — Using it for finished single actions:** ❌ `I have been visiting Paris in 2020.` → ✅ `I visited Paris in 2020. / I have visited Paris.`
- **TR:** Tek seferlik bitmis olayda continuous kullanma.

- **Tuzak 2 — Stative verbs in continuous:** ❌ `I have been knowing him for years.` → ✅ `I have known him for years.`
- **TR:** know, like, love, want, believe genelde -ing almaz.

- **Tuzak 3 — Forgetting "been":** ❌ `I have waiting for an hour.` → ✅ `I have been waiting for an hour.`
- **TR:** been olmadan cumle yanlis olur.


## 10 example sentences

- **I have been waiting for you for an hour.** — Bir saattir seni bekliyorum.
- **She has been studying English since January.** — Ocak ayindan beri Ingilizce calisiyor.
- **How long have you been working here?.** — Ne kadar suredir burada calisiyorsun?
- **They have been playing football all afternoon.** — Butun ogleden sonra futbol oynuyorlar.
- **My eyes hurt because I have been reading for hours.** — Gozlerim aciyor cunku saatlerdir okuyorum.
- **He has been living in Ankara for five years.** — Bes yildir Ankara'da yasiyor.
- **It has been raining since morning.** — Sabahtan beri yagmur yagiyor.
- **We have been trying to call you all day.** — Butun gun seni aramaya calisiyoruz.
- **She has been cooking, that's why the kitchen is messy.** — Yemek yapiyordu, mutfak o yuzden daginik.
- **How long has it been snowing?.** — Ne kadar suredir kar yagiyor?


## Quick check

- **Soru 1:** I ___ (wait) for two hours. → I have been waiting for two hours.
- **Soru 2:** ___ you ___ (learn) English since 2020? → Have you been learning English since 2020?
- **Soru 3:** Fix: "I have been knowing her for years." → I have known her for years.
- **Soru 4:** Simple or continuous? "Result finished: I have ___ (paint) the wall." → painted.
- **Soru 5:** She has been working here ___ March. (for/since) → since.
- **Soru 6:** Fix: "I have waiting long." → I have been waiting long.
- **Soru 7:** "How long ___ she ___ (live) here?" → has she been living.
- **Soru 8:** Stative verb check: love → simple (have loved), not continuous.
