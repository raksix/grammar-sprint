# B1-08 Modal Deduction

## Goal

- **Not:** Make logical guesses with must (sure), can't (impossible), might/may (possible).
- **Not:** Separate deduction (tahmin) from obligation (zorunluluk).
- **TR:** Kanita dayali tahmin yurutmek (olmalidir, olamaz, olabilir).


## Rule in 60 seconds

- **Present deduction:** **must/can't/might/may + base verb**.
- **Past deduction:** **must/can't/might have + V3**.
- **TR:** Eminsen must, imkansizsa can't, supheliysen might/may.


## Forms

| Certainty | Present | Past |
|---|---|---|
| 95% sure (yes) | He must be tired. | He must have been tired. |
| 95% sure (no) | She can't be at home. | She can't have left yet. |
| 50% possible | They might be late. | They might have missed the bus. |
| 50% possible | It may rain. | It may have rained. |

- **must (deduction) ≠ must (obligation):** "He must be rich" (tahmin) vs "I must wear uniform" (zorunluluk).
- **TR:** Cumle kanita dayaniyorsa tahmin, kurala dayaniyorsa zorunluluk.
- **Not:** Negative deduction uses can't, not mustn't: "He can't be serious."
- **TR:** Tahminde olumsuzluk can't ile kurulur.

- **Örnek:** `must + V (eminim) / can't + V (imkansiz) / might + V (belki)`
- **Örnek:** `She MUST BE at work — her car is here.`
- **Örnek:** `He CAN'T BE 40 — he looks 25.`
- **Örnek:** `They MIGHT BE lost — let's call them.`
- **Örnek:** `PAST: She MUST HAVE FORGOTTEN. / They CAN'T HAVE ARRIVED yet.`


## 3 classic traps

- **Tuzak 1 — Mustn't for deduction:** ❌ `He mustn't be at home. (means "forbidden", not "impossible")` → ✅ `He can't be at home.`
- **TR:** Tahminde olumsuzluk can't, yasaklamada mustn't.

- **Tuzak 2 — Must vs have to confusion:**
- **Deduction:** She must be tired. (I guess so)
- **Obligation:** She has to work late. (her boss says so)
- **TR:** have to ile tahmin yapilmaz.

- **Tuzak 3 — Forgetting have + V3 for past guesses:** ❌ `He must finished early.` → ✅ `He must have finished early.`
- **TR:** Gecmis tahmini icin have + V3 sart.


## 10 example sentences

- **She must be tired after that long trip.** — O uzun yolculuktan sonra yorgun olmali.
- **He can't be at home; the lights are off.** — Evde olamaz, isiklar kapali.
- **They might be stuck in traffic.** — Trafige takilmis olabilirler.
- **It may rain this evening.** — Bu aksam yagmur yagabilir.
- **You must have been shocked by the news.** — Haber seni soke etmis olmali.
- **She can't have said that; she is too polite.** — Oyle demis olamaz, cok kibardir.
- **He might have forgotten the meeting.** — Toplantiyi unutmus olabilir.
- **This must be the right address.** — Burası dogru adres olmali.
- **They can't have arrived yet; the flight just landed.** — Henuz varmis olamazlar, ucak yeni indi.
- **You must try this dessert; it's amazing.** — Bu tatliyi denemelisin, muhtesem. (tavsiye/zorunluluk tonu)


## Quick check

- **Soru 1:** Lights are off → He ___ be at home. (must/can't) → can't.
- **Soru 2:** She has three cars → She ___ be rich. → must.
- **Soru 3:** Fix: "He mustn't be serious." (deduction) → He can't be serious.
- **Soru 4:** Maybe 50%: They ___ be late. → might/may.
- **Soru 5:** Past guess: She ___ have forgotten. (must) → must.
- **Soru 6:** Impossible past: They ___ have arrived yet. → can't.
- **Soru 7:** Deduction or obligation? "I must wear a tie at work." → obligation.
- **Soru 8:** Fix: "He must finished." → He must have finished.
