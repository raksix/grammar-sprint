# B1-08 Modal Deduction

## Goal

- Make logical guesses with must (sure), can't (impossible), might/may (possible).
- Separate deduction (tahmin) from obligation (zorunluluk).
- TR: Kanita dayali tahmin yurutmek (olmalidir, olamaz, olabilir).

## Rule in 60 seconds

- Present deduction: **must/can't/might/may + base verb**.
- Past deduction: **must/can't/might have + V3**.
- TR: Eminsen must, imkansizsa can't, supheliysen might/may.

## Forms

| Certainty | Present | Past |
|---|---|---|
| 95% sure (yes) | He must be tired. | He must have been tired. |
| 95% sure (no) | She can't be at home. | She can't have left yet. |
| 50% possible | They might be late. | They might have missed the bus. |
| 50% possible | It may rain. | It may have rained. |

- must (deduction) ≠ must (obligation): "He must be rich" (tahmin) vs "I must wear uniform" (zorunluluk).
- TR: Cumle kanita dayaniyorsa tahmin, kurala dayaniyorsa zorunluluk.
- Negative deduction uses can't, not mustn't: "He can't be serious."
- TR: Tahminde olumsuzluk can't ile kurulur.

```text
must + V (eminim) / can't + V (imkansiz) / might + V (belki)
She MUST BE at work — her car is here.
He CAN'T BE 40 — he looks 25.
They MIGHT BE lost — let's call them.
PAST: She MUST HAVE FORGOTTEN. / They CAN'T HAVE ARRIVED yet.
```

## 3 classic traps

### 1. Mustn't for deduction

- Wrong: He mustn't be at home. (means "forbidden", not "impossible")
- Right: He can't be at home.
- TR: Tahminde olumsuzluk can't, yasaklamada mustn't.

### 2. Must vs have to confusion

- Deduction: She must be tired. (I guess so)
- Obligation: She has to work late. (her boss says so)
- TR: have to ile tahmin yapilmaz.

### 3. Forgetting have + V3 for past guesses

- Wrong: He must finished early.
- Right: He must have finished early.
- TR: Gecmis tahmini icin have + V3 sart.

## 10 example sentences

1. She must be tired after that long trip. — O uzun yolculuktan sonra yorgun olmali.
2. He can't be at home; the lights are off. — Evde olamaz, isiklar kapali.
3. They might be stuck in traffic. — Trafige takilmis olabilirler.
4. It may rain this evening. — Bu aksam yagmur yagabilir.
5. You must have been shocked by the news. — Haber seni soke etmis olmali.
6. She can't have said that; she is too polite. — Oyle demis olamaz, cok kibardir.
7. He might have forgotten the meeting. — Toplantiyi unutmus olabilir.
8. This must be the right address. — Burası dogru adres olmali.
9. They can't have arrived yet; the flight just landed. — Henuz varmis olamazlar, ucak yeni indi.
10. You must try this dessert; it's amazing. — Bu tatliyi denemelisin, muhtesem. (tavsiye/zorunluluk tonu)

## Quick check

1. Lights are off → He ___ be at home. (must/can't) → can't.
2. She has three cars → She ___ be rich. → must.
3. Fix: "He mustn't be serious." (deduction) → He can't be serious.
4. Maybe 50%: They ___ be late. → might/may.
5. Past guess: She ___ have forgotten. (must) → must.
6. Impossible past: They ___ have arrived yet. → can't.
7. Deduction or obligation? "I must wear a tie at work." → obligation.
8. Fix: "He must finished." → He must have finished.
