# B1-06 Passive Basic

## Goal

- **Not:** Build present and past simple passive with be + V3.
- **Not:** Decide when to include or drop the by-agent.
- **TR:** Eylemi yapan degil, eylemden etkilenen onemliyse passive kullan.


## Rule in 60 seconds

- **Form:** **be + past participle (V3)**. Tense lives in "be".
- **Present:** is/are + V3. Past: was/were + V3.
- **TR:** Etken ozne belli degilse ya da onemsizse passive dogaldir.


## Forms

| Tense | Active | Passive |
|---|---|---|
| Present simple | They make cars. | Cars are made here. |
| Past simple | They built it in 2000. | It was built in 2000. |
| Negative | They didn't invite us. | We weren't invited. |
| Question | Did they cancel it? | Was it cancelled? |

- **Not:** Agent with `by` only when the doer is important or surprising:
- **Not:** "The bridge was designed by a young engineer."
- **TR:** Yapan kisi herkesce belliyse by kismini at.
- **Spelling:** cancelled, stopped, written, built — V3 forms matter.
- **Questions:** When was it built? Who was it written by?

- **Örnek:** `be + V3 (tense in BE)`
- **Örnek:** `English IS SPOKEN here. / The road WAS CLOSED yesterday.`
- **Örnek:** `The windows WEREN'T CLEANED. / WAS the thief CAUGHT?`


## 3 classic traps

- **Tuzak 1 — Wrong be-verb for the subject:** ❌ `The cars was repaired.` → ✅ `The cars were repaired.`
- **TR:** be fiili yeni ozneye (passive ozne) uyar.

- **Tuzak 2 — Using active word order with passive verb:** ❌ `Was built the bridge in 1990?` → ✅ `Was the bridge built in 1990?`
- **TR:** Soru dizilimi: Was/Were + ozne + V3.

- **Tuzak 3 — Keeping an unnecessary by-agent:**
- **Weak:** The bread was baked by the baker. (obvious)
- **Better:** The bread was baked this morning.
- **TR:** Bariz faili soylemek gereksiz; zaman/yer bilgisi daha faydali.


## 10 example sentences

- **English is spoken in many countries.** — Ingilizce bircok ulkede konusulur.
- **The bridge was built in 1995.** — Kopru 1995'te insa edildi.
- **The windows aren't cleaned every day.** — Pencereler her gun temizlenmez.
- **Was the thief caught last night?.** — Hirsiz dun gece yakalandi mi?
- **This car was made in Germany.** — Bu araba Almanya'da uretildi.
- **The homework wasn't checked yesterday.** — Odev dun kontrol edilmedi.
- **Turkish coffee is served in small cups.** — Turk kahvesi kucuk fincanlarda servis edilir.
- **The match was cancelled because of rain.** — Mac yagmur yuzunden iptal edildi.
- **Were you invited to the party?.** — Partiye davet edildin mi?
- **The museum is visited by thousands every year.** — Muze her yil binlerce kisi tarafindan ziyaret edilir.


## Quick check

- **Soru 1:** The room ___ (clean) every day. → is cleaned.
- **Soru 2:** The letter ___ (send) yesterday. → was sent.
- **Soru 3:** Fix: "The books was printed." → The books were printed.
- **Soru 4:** ___ the road closed last week? (Was/Were) → Was.
- **Soru 5:** Active → passive: "They grow tea here." → Tea is grown here.
- **Soru 6:** Active → passive: "She wrote the report." → The report was written.
- **Soru 7:** Fix: "Was built the school in 2000?" → Was the school built in 2000?
- **Soru 8:** Drop or keep? "Rice is grown by farmers." → drop (obvious).
