# B1-06 Passive Basic

## Goal

- Build present and past simple passive with be + V3.
- Decide when to include or drop the by-agent.
- TR: Eylemi yapan degil, eylemden etkilenen onemliyse passive kullan.

## Rule in 60 seconds

- Form: **be + past participle (V3)**. Tense lives in "be".
- Present: is/are + V3. Past: was/were + V3.
- TR: Etken ozne belli degilse ya da onemsizse passive dogaldir.

## Forms

| Tense | Active | Passive |
|---|---|---|
| Present simple | They make cars. | Cars are made here. |
| Past simple | They built it in 2000. | It was built in 2000. |
| Negative | They didn't invite us. | We weren't invited. |
| Question | Did they cancel it? | Was it cancelled? |

- Agent with `by` only when the doer is important or surprising:
  - "The bridge was designed by a young engineer."
- TR: Yapan kisi herkesce belliyse by kismini at.
- Spelling: cancelled, stopped, written, built — V3 forms matter.
- Questions: When was it built? Who was it written by?

```text
be + V3 (tense in BE)
English IS SPOKEN here. / The road WAS CLOSED yesterday.
The windows WEREN'T CLEANED. / WAS the thief CAUGHT?
```

## 3 classic traps

### 1. Wrong be-verb for the subject

- Wrong: The cars was repaired.
- Right: The cars were repaired.
- TR: be fiili yeni ozneye (passive ozne) uyar.

### 2. Using active word order with passive verb

- Wrong: Was built the bridge in 1990?
- Right: Was the bridge built in 1990?
- TR: Soru dizilimi: Was/Were + ozne + V3.

### 3. Keeping an unnecessary by-agent

- Weak: The bread was baked by the baker. (obvious)
- Better: The bread was baked this morning.
- TR: Bariz faili soylemek gereksiz; zaman/yer bilgisi daha faydali.

## 10 example sentences

1. English is spoken in many countries. — Ingilizce bircok ulkede konusulur.
2. The bridge was built in 1995. — Kopru 1995'te insa edildi.
3. The windows aren't cleaned every day. — Pencereler her gun temizlenmez.
4. Was the thief caught last night? — Hirsiz dun gece yakalandi mi?
5. This car was made in Germany. — Bu araba Almanya'da uretildi.
6. The homework wasn't checked yesterday. — Odev dun kontrol edilmedi.
7. Turkish coffee is served in small cups. — Turk kahvesi kucuk fincanlarda servis edilir.
8. The match was cancelled because of rain. — Mac yagmur yuzunden iptal edildi.
9. Were you invited to the party? — Partiye davet edildin mi?
10. The museum is visited by thousands every year. — Muze her yil binlerce kisi tarafindan ziyaret edilir.

## Quick check

1. The room ___ (clean) every day. → is cleaned.
2. The letter ___ (send) yesterday. → was sent.
3. Fix: "The books was printed." → The books were printed.
4. ___ the road closed last week? (Was/Were) → Was.
5. Active → passive: "They grow tea here." → Tea is grown here.
6. Active → passive: "She wrote the report." → The report was written.
7. Fix: "Was built the school in 2000?" → Was the school built in 2000?
8. Drop or keep? "Rice is grown by farmers." → drop (obvious).
