# B1-10 Relative Clauses Defining

## Goal

- Build defining relative clauses with who/which/that/whose/where/when.
- Drop the object pronoun correctly (the book I bought).
- TR: Ismi niteleyen yan cumleler kurmak (tanımlayan).

## Rule in 60 seconds

- Defining clause = essential information, no commas.
- who (people), which (things), that (both), whose (possession), where (place), when (time).
- TR: Hangi sorusunun cevabi sartsa defining, virgul yok.

## Forms

| Pronoun | Use | Example |
|---|---|---|
| who / that | people (subject) | The man who called is my uncle. |
| which / that | things (subject) | The car which broke down is fixed. |
| whom / who / that / — | people (object) | The girl (who) I met is kind. |
| which / that / — | things (object) | The book (that) I bought is great. |
| whose | possession | The boy whose bike was stolen cried. |
| where | place | The city where I was born is small. |
| when | time | The day when we met was sunny. |

- Object pronouns can be dropped: "The film (that) we watched was long."
- TR: Nesne zamirini atabilirsin; ozneyi atamazsin.
- Wrong drop: "The man called you is here." (who = subject, cannot drop)
- Right: "The man who called you is here."

```text
SUBJECT (can't drop): The woman WHO lives next door is a doctor.
OBJECT (can drop): The cake (THAT) you made was delicious.
WHOSE: a friend WHOSE father is a pilot. / WHERE: the cafe WHERE we met.
```

## 3 classic traps

### 1. Dropping a subject pronoun

- Wrong: The people live here are friendly.
- Right: The people who live here are friendly.
- TR: Ozne konumundaki who/which/that atilmaz.

### 2. Commas in defining clauses

- Wrong: My brother, who lives in Berlin, is an engineer. (this = non-defining, different meaning)
- Right: My brother who lives in Berlin is an engineer. (I have more than one brother)
- TR: Defining'de virgul yok.

### 3. What as a relative pronoun

- Wrong: The book what I bought...
- Right: The book that/which I bought...
- TR: what baglac olarak kullanilmaz; that/which kullan.

## 10 example sentences

1. The man who helped us was very kind. — Bize yardim eden adam cok nazikti.
2. The phone that I bought is already broken. — Aldigim telefon coktan bozuldu.
3. She visited the village where she was born. — Dogdugu koyu ziyaret etti.
4. The student whose homework was missing apologised. — Odevi eksik olan ogrenci ozur diledi.
5. Do you remember the day when we first met? — Ilk tanistigimiz gunu hatirliyor musun?
6. The restaurant which we tried last night was amazing. — Dun gece denedigimiz restoran muhtesemdi.
7. Anyone who wants to join must sign up. — Katilmak isteyen herkes kaydolmalı.
8. The movie we watched was too long. — Izledigimiz film cok uzundu. (that dropped)
9. He has a friend whose sister is famous. — Kiz kardesi unlu olan bir arkadasi var.
10. This is the reason that I called you. — Seni arama sebebim bu.

## Quick check

1. The woman ___ lives there is a nurse. (who/—) → who (subject, keep).
2. The book ___ I bought... (drop ok?) → (that) — yes, object.
3. Fix: "The people live here are nice." → The people who live here...
4. The boy ___ bike was stolen... → whose.
5. The day ___ we met... → when.
6. The city ___ I grew up... → where.
7. Fix: "The car what I sold..." → that/which I sold.
8. Join or not? "The house, which is old, is mine." → non-defining (commas) — different lesson.
