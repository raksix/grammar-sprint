# B1-10 Relative Clauses Defining

## Goal

- **Not:** Build defining relative clauses with who/which/that/whose/where/when.
- **Not:** Drop the object pronoun correctly (the book I bought).
- **TR:** Ismi niteleyen yan cumleler kurmak (tanımlayan).


## Rule in 60 seconds

- **Not:** Defining clause = essential information, no commas.
- **Not:** who (people), which (things), that (both), whose (possession), where (place), when (time).
- **TR:** Hangi sorusunun cevabi sartsa defining, virgul yok.


## Forms

| Pronoun | Use | Example |
|---|---|---|
| who / that | people (subject) | The man who called is my uncle. |
| which / that | things (subject) | The car which broke down is fixed. |
| whom / who / that / — | people (object) | The girl (who) I met is kind. |
| which / that / — | things (object) | The book (that) I bought is great. |
| whose | possession | The boy whose bike was stolen cried. |
| where | place | The city where I was born is small. |
| when | time | The day when we met was sunny. |

- **Object pronouns can be dropped:** "The film (that) we watched was long."
- **TR:** Nesne zamirini atabilirsin; ozneyi atamazsin.
- **Wrong drop:** "The man called you is here." (who = subject, cannot drop)
- **Right:** "The man who called you is here."

- **Örnek:** `SUBJECT (can't drop): The woman WHO lives next door is a doctor.`
- **Örnek:** `OBJECT (can drop): The cake (THAT) you made was delicious.`
- **Örnek:** `WHOSE: a friend WHOSE father is a pilot. / WHERE: the cafe WHERE we met.`


## 3 classic traps

- **Tuzak 1 — Dropping a subject pronoun:** ❌ `The people live here are friendly.` → ✅ `The people who live here are friendly.`
- **TR:** Ozne konumundaki who/which/that atilmaz.

- **Tuzak 2 — Commas in defining clauses:**
- ❌ **Yanlış:** `My brother, who lives in Berlin, is an engineer. (this = non-defining, different meaning)`
- ✅ **Doğru:** `My brother who lives in Berlin is an engineer. (I have more than one brother)`
- **TR:** Defining'de virgul yok.

- **Tuzak 3 — What as a relative pronoun:** ❌ `The book what I bought...` → ✅ `The book that/which I bought...`
- **TR:** what baglac olarak kullanilmaz; that/which kullan.


## 10 example sentences

- **The man who helped us was very kind.** — Bize yardim eden adam cok nazikti.
- **The phone that I bought is already broken.** — Aldigim telefon coktan bozuldu.
- **She visited the village where she was born.** — Dogdugu koyu ziyaret etti.
- **The student whose homework was missing apologised.** — Odevi eksik olan ogrenci ozur diledi.
- **Do you remember the day when we first met?.** — Ilk tanistigimiz gunu hatirliyor musun?
- **The restaurant which we tried last night was amazing.** — Dun gece denedigimiz restoran muhtesemdi.
- **Anyone who wants to join must sign up.** — Katilmak isteyen herkes kaydolmalı.
- **The movie we watched was too long.** — Izledigimiz film cok uzundu. (that dropped)
- **He has a friend whose sister is famous.** — Kiz kardesi unlu olan bir arkadasi var.
- **This is the reason that I called you.** — Seni arama sebebim bu.


## Quick check

- **Soru 1:** The woman ___ lives there is a nurse. (who/—) → who (subject, keep).
- **Soru 2:** The book ___ I bought... (drop ok?) → (that) — yes, object.
- **Soru 3:** Fix: "The people live here are nice." → The people who live here...
- **Soru 4:** The boy ___ bike was stolen... → whose.
- **Soru 5:** The day ___ we met... → when.
- **Soru 6:** The city ___ I grew up... → where.
- **Soru 7:** Fix: "The car what I sold..." → that/which I sold.
- **Soru 8:** Join or not? "The house, which is old, is mine." → non-defining (commas) — different lesson.
