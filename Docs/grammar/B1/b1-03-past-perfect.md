# B1-03 Past Perfect

## Goal

- Use had + V3 to show one past action happened before another past action.
- Use before/after/by the time correctly in stories.
- TR: Gecmiste iki olayin hangisinin once oldugunu gostermek.

## Rule in 60 seconds

- Form: **had + V3** for all subjects (I had left, they had eaten).
- The earlier action takes past perfect; the later action stays in past simple.
- TR: Once olan had + V3, sonra olan normal past simple.

## Forms

| Pattern | Example |
|---|---|
| Affirmative | I had finished before he arrived. |
| Negative | I hadn't finished when he arrived. |
| Question | Had you finished before he arrived? |

- Same form for every subject: I/you/he/she/we/they + had.
- TR: Ozne ne olursa olsun had degismez.
- Key connectors: `before`, `after`, `by the time`, `already`, `just`, `never`.
- Order does not matter: "When I arrived, the train had left." = "The train had left before I arrived."

```text
had + V3 (all subjects)
When I got home, the kids HAD already EATEN.
She HAD never SEEN snow BEFORE that trip.
BY THE TIME we arrived, the film HAD started.
```

## 3 classic traps

### 1. Using past perfect for a single past event

- Wrong: I had seen him yesterday.
- Right: I saw him yesterday.
- TR: Tek olay varsa past simple yeter; iki olay karsilastirmasi lazim.

### 2. Putting both verbs in past perfect

- Wrong: When I had arrived, the train had left.
- Right: When I arrived, the train had left.
- TR: Sadece once olan had alir, digeri past simple kalir.

### 3. After + past perfect confusion

- After I had finished = bitirdikten sonra (correct).
- Wrong: After I finished, I had gone home. (mixing without need)
- Right: After I finished, I went home. / I had finished before I went home.
- TR: after zaten sirayi gosterir; had'i gereksiz yere ikinci fiile koyma.

## 10 example sentences

1. When I arrived, the train had already left. — Vardigimda tren coktan gitmisti.
2. She was hungry because she hadn't eaten breakfast. — Acikmisti cunku kahvalti etmemisti.
3. Had you finished your homework before dinner? — Aksam yemeginden once odevini bitirmis miydin?
4. By the time we got to the cinema, the film had started. — Sinemaya vardigimizda film baslamisti.
5. I recognised him because I had seen him before. — Onu tanidim cunku daha once gormustum.
6. They had never flown before that holiday. — O tatilden once hic ucmamislardi.
7. After she had locked the door, she left. — Kapiyi kilitledikten sonra cikti.
8. He failed because he hadn't studied. — Basarisiz oldu cunku calismamisti.
9. I had just fallen asleep when the phone rang. — Tam uykuya dalmistim ki telefon caldi.
10. She told me she had lost her wallet. — Cuzdanini kaybettigini soyledi.

## Quick check

1. When I arrived, they ___ (leave). → had left.
2. She was tired because she ___ (not / sleep) well. → hadn't slept.
3. Fix: "I had met him last year." (single event) → I met him last year.
4. ___ you ___ (finish) before 8 pm? → Had you finished.
5. By the time we arrived, the show ___ (start). → had started.
6. Which is earlier? "She had cooked before I came." → cooked.
7. Fix: "When I had arrived, he had gone." → When I arrived, he had gone.
8. After they ___ (eat), they went out. → ate / had eaten (both ok, simple preferred).
