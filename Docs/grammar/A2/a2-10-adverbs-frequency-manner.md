# Adverbs of Frequency and Manner

## Goal

- Use frequency adverbs (always → never) and manner adverbs (-ly) in the correct word order.
- (TR: Sıklık zarflarını ve -ly ile yapılan durum zarflarını doğru sırada kullanmak.)

## Rule in 60 seconds

- Frequency scale: **always (100%) – usually – often – sometimes – rarely / seldom – hardly ever – never (0%)**.
- Frequency position: **before** the main verb, **after** be: *She always drinks tea / She is always late.*
- Manner = adjective + **-ly**: *quick → quickly, careful → carefully, slow → slowly.*
- Manner position: **after** the verb (+ object): *He drives carefully / She speaks English well.*
- (TR: Sıklık zarfı fiilden önce, be'den sonra gelir: I always go / I am always tired. Durum zarfı fiilden sonra: He runs fast.)

## Forms

| Pattern | Structure | Example |
|---|---|---|
| frequency + main verb | subj + frequency + verb | I **usually get up** at 7. |
| frequency + be | subj + be + frequency | They **are often** late. |
| frequency + auxiliary | subj + aux + frequency + verb | She **doesn't usually** watch TV. |
| manner -ly | adjective + -ly | quick → **quickly** |
| manner + verb | verb (+ object) + adverb | He **speaks quietly**. |
| irregular manner | good → well, fast → fast, hard → hard | She sings **well**. |

- Spelling: *easy → easily, happy → happily, terrible → terribly, basic → basically.*
- Adjectives ending in -ly (friendly, lovely, lonely) are NOT adverbs — use "in a friendly way".
- (TR: easy → easily, happy → happily. friendly gibi -ly biten sıfatlar zarf değildir.)
- Frequency + time expressions can combine: *I usually play football on Sundays.*
- Manner adverbs go before past participles/adjectives in formal position: *a well-known actor, a badly written text.*
- Frequency in questions: *How often do you swim? — Twice a week / Every day.*
- never + affirmative verb (no "not"): *She never smokes* (not "she doesn't never smoke").
- (TR: never olumlu fiille kullanılır: never + not yanlış. Sıklık sorusu: How often…?)

## 3 classic traps

1. **Wrong frequency position:** ❌ *I go always to school.* → ✅ *I always go to school.* (TR: Sıklık zarfı fiilden önce gelir.)
2. **Adjective instead of adverb:** ❌ *She drives careful.* → ✅ *She drives carefully.* (TR: Fiili niteleyen sözcük zarf olur: -ly.)
3. **good vs well:** ❌ *He speaks good English.* (as adverb) → ✅ *He speaks English well.* (TR: Fiilden sonra good değil well: speaks well.)

## 10 example sentences

1. I **always** have breakfast at home. (Her zaman evde kahvaltı yaparım.)
2. She is **never** late for work. (İşe asla geç kalmaz.)
3. They **usually** play football on Sundays. (Genelde pazar günleri futbol oynarlar.)
4. He **sometimes** watches films in English. (Bazen İngilizce film izler.)
5. We **hardly ever** eat fast food. (Neredeyse hiç fast food yemeyiz.)
6. She speaks English **fluently**. (İngilizceyi akıcı konuşur.)
7. He drives **carefully** in the rain. (Yağmurda dikkatli araba sürer.)
8. The baby is sleeping **quietly**. (Bebek sessizce uyuyor.)
9. She did **well** in the exam. (Sınavda iyi yaptı.)
10. Please listen **carefully** to the instructions. (Lütfen talimatları dikkatle dinleyin.)

## Quick check

1. Put in order: "go / usually / I / to school / by bus."
2. Fill in: She dances ___ (beautiful).
3. Correct the error: *They are late always.*
4. Choose: "He plays tennis ___." (good / well)
5. True or false: "She hardly ever drinks coffee" means she drinks coffee often.
6. Rewrite with the adverb in the correct place: "She goes always by bus."

<details>
<summary>Answers</summary>

1. I usually go to school by bus.
2. beautifully
3. They are always late. (be + frequency)
4. well
5. False — hardly ever = almost never.
6. She always goes by bus.
