# Adverbs of Frequency and Manner

## Goal


- **Use** frequency adverbs (always → never) and manner adverbs (-ly) in the correct word order.
- **TR:** Sıklık zarflarını ve -ly ile yapılan durum zarflarını doğru sırada kullanmak.

- **Success check:** describe your week with always, usually and never.


## Rule in 60 seconds


- **Frequency scale:** **always (100%) – usually – often – sometimes – rarely / seldom – hardly ever – never (0%)**.
- **Frequency position:** **before** the main verb, **after** be: *She always drinks tea / She is always late.*
- **Manner** = adjective + **-ly**: *quick → quickly, careful → carefully, slow → slowly.*
- **Manner position:** **after** the verb (+ object): *He drives carefully / She speaks English well.*
- **TR:** Sıklık zarfı fiilden önce, be'den sonra gelir: I always go / I am always tired. Durum zarfı fiilden sonra: He runs fast.

- **Double-check:** find the verb first, then place the adverb.


## Forms


| Pattern | Structure | Example |
|---|---|---|
| frequency + main verb | subj + frequency + verb | I **usually get up** at 7. |
| frequency + be | subj + be + frequency | They **are often** late. |
| frequency + auxiliary | subj + aux + frequency + verb | She **doesn't usually** watch TV. |
| manner -ly | adjective + -ly | quick → **quickly** |
| manner + verb | verb (+ object) + adverb | He **speaks quietly**. |
| irregular manner | good → well, fast → fast, hard → hard | She sings **well**. |

- **Spelling:** *easy → easily, happy → happily, terrible → terribly, basic → basically.*
- **Adjectives** ending in -ly (friendly, lovely, lonely) are NOT adverbs — use "in a friendly way".
- **TR:** easy → easily, happy → happily. friendly gibi -ly biten sıfatlar zarf değildir.
- **Frequency + time expressions can combine:** *I usually play football on Sundays.*
- **Manner** adverbs go before past participles/adjectives in formal position: *a well-known actor, a badly written text.*
- **Frequency in questions:** *How often do you swim? — Twice a week / Every day.*
- **never + affirmative verb (no "not"):** *She never smokes* (not "she doesn't never smoke").
- **TR:** never olumlu fiille kullanılır: never + not yanlış. Sıklık sorusu: How often…?

- **How often:** Twice a week and Every day answer frequency questions.


## 3 classic traps


- **Wrong frequency position:** ❌ *I go always to school.* → ✅ *I always go to school.* (TR: Sıklık zarfı fiilden önce gelir.)
- **Adjective instead of adverb:** ❌ *She drives careful.* → ✅ *She drives carefully.* (TR: Fiili niteleyen sözcük zarf olur: -ly.)
- **good vs well:** ❌ *He speaks good English.* (as adverb) → ✅ *He speaks English well.* (TR: Fiilden sonra good değil well: speaks well.)


## 10 example sentences


- **I always have breakfast at home.** — Her zaman evde kahvaltı yaparım.
- **She is never late for work.** — İşe asla geç kalmaz.
- **They usually play football on Sundays.** — Genelde pazar günleri futbol oynarlar.
- **He sometimes watches films in English.** — Bazen İngilizce film izler.
- **We hardly ever eat fast food.** — Neredeyse hiç fast food yemeyiz.
- **She speaks English fluently.** — İngilizceyi akıcı konuşur.
- **He drives carefully in the rain.** — Yağmurda dikkatli araba sürer.
- **The baby is sleeping quietly.** — Bebek sessizce uyuyor.
- **She did well in the exam.** — Sınavda iyi yaptı.
- **Please listen carefully to the instructions.** — Lütfen talimatları dikkatle dinleyin.


## Quick check


- **Q1:** Put in order: "go / usually / I / to school / by bus."
- **Q2:** Fill in: She dances ___ (beautiful).
- **Q3:** Correct the error: *They are late always.*
- **Q4:** Choose: "He plays tennis ___." (good / well)
- **Q5:** True or false: "She hardly ever drinks coffee" means she drinks coffee often.
- **Q6:** Rewrite with the adverb in the correct place: "She goes always by bus."

<details>
<summary>Answers</summary>

- **A1:** I usually go to school by bus.
- **A2:** beautifully
- **A3:** They are always late. (be + frequency)
- **A4:** well
- **A5:** False — hardly ever = almost never.
- **A6:** She always goes by bus.

</details>
