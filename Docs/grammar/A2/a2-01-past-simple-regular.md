# Past Simple: Regular Verbs

## Goal

- Use past simple regular verbs to talk about finished actions in the past.
- (TR: Geçmişte bitmiş eylemleri düzenli fiillerle anlatmak.)

## Rule in 60 seconds

- Add **-ed** to the base verb for all persons: I worked, she worked, they worked.
- Use finished-time words: yesterday, last night, two days ago, in 2019.
- Negative: **did not (didn't) + base verb**. Question: **Did + subject + base verb?**
- (TR: Olumlu cümlede fiile -ed gelir, kişi fark etmez. Olumsuzda didn't + yalın fiil, soruda Did + özne + yalın fiil.)

## Forms

| Type | Structure | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Affirmative | S + V-ed | I watched a film. |
| Negative | S + did not + V | I did not watch a film. |
| Question | Did + S + V? | Did you watch a film? |
| Short answer | Yes, S + did / No, S + didn't | Yes, I did. / No, I didn't. |

- Spelling rules:
  - Most verbs: + -ed → play → played, clean → cleaned.
  - Verb ends in -e: + -d → live → lived, dance → danced.
  - Consonant + y: y → i + -ed → study → studied, carry → carried. (Vowel + y keeps y: play → played.)
  - Short verb, single vowel + single consonant: double the consonant → stop → stopped, plan → planned.
- Pronunciation of -ed: /t/ (helped), /d/ (played), /ɪd/ (wanted, started).
- (TR: -e ile biten fiile sadece -d; ünsüz + y → -ied; kısa fiillerde ünsüz ikizlenir. -ed üç farklı sesle okunur.)

## 3 classic traps

1. **Double past marking:** ❌ *I didn't watched.* → ✅ *I didn't watch.* (TR: didn't varsa fiil yalın kalır, -ed düşer.)
2. **did in affirmative:** ❌ *I did watched.* → ✅ *I watched.* / *I did watch.* (TR: Olumlu cümlede did vurgu içindir, fiil yine yalındır.)
3. **Present tense for finished past:** ❌ *Yesterday I watch a film.* → ✅ *Yesterday I watched a film.* (TR: yesterday/last/ago varsa fiil geçmiş olmalı.)

## 10 example sentences

1. I **cleaned** my room yesterday. (Dün odamı temizledim.)
2. She **worked** late last night. (Dün gece geç saate kadar çalıştı.)
3. We **watched** a comedy last weekend. (Geçen hafta sonu bir komedi izledik.)
4. They **played** football two days ago. (İki gün önce futbol oynadılar.)
5. He **studied** English in 2020. (2020'de İngilizce çalıştı.)
6. I **didn't like** the food. (Yemeği beğenmedim.)
7. She **didn't call** me back. (Beni geri aramadı.)
8. **Did you enjoy** the party? (Partiden keyif aldın mı?)
9. **Did they arrive** on time? (Zamanında vardılar mı?)
10. We **stopped** at a small café and **tasted** local food. (Küçük bir kafede durduk ve yöresel yemek tattık.)

## Quick check

1. Fill in: I ___ (watch) a great series last night.
2. Correct the error: *She didn't played tennis.*
3. Make a question: you / visit / your grandparents / last Sunday?
4. True or false: "I have watched a film yesterday." is correct past simple.
5. Pronounce: played, wanted, helped — which ends in /ɪd/?

<details>
<summary>Answers</summary>

1. watched
2. She didn't play tennis.
3. Did you visit your grandparents last Sunday?
4. False — "yesterday" needs past simple: "I watched a film yesterday."
5. wanted
