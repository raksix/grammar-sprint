# Past Simple: Regular Verbs

## Goal


- **Use** past simple regular verbs to talk about finished actions in the past.
- **TR:** Geçmişte bitmiş eylemleri düzenli fiillerle anlatmak.

- **Success check:** say three things you did yesterday using regular verbs.


## Rule in 60 seconds


- **Add** **-ed** to the base verb for all persons: I worked, she worked, they worked.
- **Use finished-time words:** yesterday, last night, two days ago, in 2019.
- **Negative:** **did not (didn't) + base verb**. Question: **Did + subject + base verb?**
- **TR:** Olumlu cümlede fiile -ed gelir, kişi fark etmez. Olumsuzda didn't + yalın fiil, soruda Did + özne + yalın fiil.

- **Same form:** I, you, he, she, we, they all take V-ed.

- **Helper did:** negatives and questions always use did + base verb.

- **Quick test:** see yesterday or last night → use V-ed.


## Forms


| Type | Structure | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Affirmative | S + V-ed | I watched a film. |
| Negative | S + did not + V | I did not watch a film. |
| Question | Did + S + V? | Did you watch a film? |
| Short answer | Yes, S + did / No, S + didn't | Yes, I did. / No, I didn't. |

- **Most verbs:** + -ed → play → played, clean → cleaned.
- **Verb ends in -e:** + -d → live → lived, dance → danced.
- **Consonant + y:** y → i + -ed → study → studied, carry → carried. (Vowel + y keeps y: play → played.)
- **Short verb, single vowel + single consonant:** double the consonant → stop → stopped, plan → planned.
- **Pronunciation of -ed:** /t/ (helped), /d/ (played), /ɪd/ (wanted, started).
- **TR:** -e ile biten fiile sadece -d; ünsüz + y → -ied; kısa fiillerde ünsüz ikizlenir. -ed üç farklı sesle okunur.

- **Short answers:** Yes, I did. / No, I didn't. — never repeat the verb.

- **Word order:** time words go at the end — I worked late last night.


## 3 classic traps


- **Double past marking:** ❌ *I didn't watched.* → ✅ *I didn't watch.* (TR: didn't varsa fiil yalın kalır, -ed düşer.)
- **did in affirmative:** ❌ *I did watched.* → ✅ *I watched.* / *I did watch.* (TR: Olumlu cümlede did vurgu içindir, fiil yine yalındır.)
- **Present tense for finished past:** ❌ *Yesterday I watch a film.* → ✅ *Yesterday I watched a film.* (TR: yesterday/last/ago varsa fiil geçmiş olmalı.)


## 10 example sentences


- **I cleaned my room yesterday.** — Dün odamı temizledim.
- **She worked late last night.** — Dün gece geç saate kadar çalıştı.
- **We watched a comedy last weekend.** — Geçen hafta sonu bir komedi izledik.
- **They played football two days ago.** — İki gün önce futbol oynadılar.
- **He studied English in 2020.** — 2020'de İngilizce çalıştı.
- **I didn't like the food.** — Yemeği beğenmedim.
- **She didn't call me back.** — Beni geri aramadı.
- **Did you enjoy the party?** — Partiden keyif aldın mı?
- **Did they arrive on time?** — Zamanında vardılar mı?
- **We stopped at a small café and tasted local food.** — Küçük bir kafede durduk ve yöresel yemek tattık.


## Quick check


- **Q1:** Fill in: I ___ (watch) a great series last night.
- **Q2:** Correct the error: *She didn't played tennis.*
- **Q3:** Make a question: you / visit / your grandparents / last Sunday?
- **Q4:** True or false: "I have watched a film yesterday." is correct past simple.
- **Q5:** Pronounce: played, wanted, helped — which ends in /ɪd/?

- **Q6:** Write the past of: dance, carry, plan.
<details>
<summary>Answers</summary>

- **A1:** watched
- **A2:** She didn't play tennis.
- **A3:** Did you visit your grandparents last Sunday?
- **A4:** False — "yesterday" needs past simple: "I watched a film yesterday."
- **A5:** wanted

- **A6:** danced, carried, planned.
</details>
