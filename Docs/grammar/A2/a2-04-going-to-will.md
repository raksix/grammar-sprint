# Going To vs Will

## Goal


- **Talk about the future:** plans with **going to**, instant decisions/promises/predictions with **will**.
- **TR:** Planlar için going to, anlık kararlar/vaatler/tahminler için will kullanmak.

- **Success check:** announce one plan and make one instant offer.

- **TR:** Bu derste plan ile anlık karar ayrımını öğreneceksin.


## Rule in 60 seconds


- **be going to + V**: plans and intentions you already made; visible evidence predictions (*Look at the clouds! It's going to rain.*)
- **will + V**: decisions made while speaking, promises, offers, general predictions (*I think it will rain tomorrow.*)
- **Form never changes with person:** I will, she will; I am going to, they are going to.
- **TR:** Önceden verilmiş karar → going to. Konuşma anında karar, söz, teklif → will.

- **Evidence rule:** dark clouds now → going to; general guess → will.

- **Person test:** will never takes -s — she will, not she wills.

- **Promise words:** promise, offer and help signal will.


## Forms


| Type | going to | will |
|---|---|---|
| Affirmative | S + am/is/are + going to + V | S + will + V |
| Negative | S + am/is/are + not + going to + V | S + will not (won't) + V |
| Question | Am/Is/Are + S + going to + V? | Will + S + V? |

- **Contractions:** I'll, you'll, he'll, won't (= will not), gonna (informal spoken).
- **Time words:** tomorrow, next week, soon, this evening, in 2030.
- **TR:** won't = will not. gonna sadece konuşma dilinde.

- **Contraction drill:** I will → I'll; will not → won't.

- **Question swap:** Will you come? / Are you going to come?

- **gonna warning:** gonna is speaking only — never write it.

- **Negative pair:** I'm not going to go / I won't go — both are natural.


## 3 classic traps


- **will for fixed plans:** ❌ *I will meet my doctor tomorrow at 10.* (appointment) → ✅ *I'm going to meet my doctor tomorrow at 10.* (TR: Randevu gibi kesin plan → going to.)
- **-ing after to:** ❌ *I'm going to studying.* → ✅ *I'm going to study.* (TR: to'dan sonra yalın fiil.)
- **s on will:** ❌ *She wills come.* → ✅ *She will come.* (TR: will hiçbir kişide -s almaz.)


## 10 example sentences


- **I'm going to visit my grandmother tomorrow.** — Yarın büyükannemi ziyaret edeceğim — plan.
- **We're going to move to a new flat.** — Yeni bir daireye taşınacağız — karar verildi.
- **Look at those clouds! It's going to rain.** — Bulutlara bak! Yağmur yağacak — belirti var.
- **A: The bag is heavy. B: I'll help you.** — Anlık teklif → will.
- **I'll call you tonight, I promise.** — Söz veriyorum → will.
- **I think robots will do more jobs.** — Genel tahmin → will.
- **She won't be late.** — Geç kalmayacak.
- **Will you come with us?** — Bizimle gelir misin?
- **They're not going to buy a car this year.** — Bu yıl araba almayacaklar.
- **One day people will live on Mars.** — Bir gün insanlar Mars'ta yaşayacak.


## Quick check


- **Q1:** Fill in: Look! He ___ (fall)!
- **Q2:** Choose: A: "I forgot my wallet." B: "I ___ (lend) you money." (will / am going to)
- **Q3:** Correct the error: *She wills help us.*
- **Q4:** Which fits a fixed plan: "I ___ see the dentist at 9 a.m."?
- **Q5:** True or false: "I'm going to studying tonight." is correct.

- **Q6:** Fill in: She ___ (be) famous one day, I think.
<details>
<summary>Answers</summary>

- **A1:** is going to fall
- **A2:** will (’ll lend — instant offer)
- **A3:** She will help us.
- **A4:** ’m going to (fixed plan)
- **A5:** False — "I'm going to study tonight."

- **A6:** will be ('ll be — general prediction).
</details>
