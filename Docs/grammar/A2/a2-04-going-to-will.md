# Going To vs Will

## Goal

- Talk about the future: plans with **going to**, instant decisions/promises/predictions with **will**.
- (TR: Planlar için going to, anlık kararlar/vaatler/tahminler için will kullanmak.)

## Rule in 60 seconds

- **be going to + V**: plans and intentions you already made; visible evidence predictions (*Look at the clouds! It's going to rain.*)
- **will + V**: decisions made while speaking, promises, offers, general predictions (*I think it will rain tomorrow.*)
- Form never changes with person: I will, she will; I am going to, they are going to.
- (TR: Önceden verilmiş karar → going to. Konuşma anında karar, söz, teklif → will.)

## Forms

| Type | going to | will |
|---|---|---|
| Affirmative | S + am/is/are + going to + V | S + will + V |
| Negative | S + am/is/are + not + going to + V | S + will not (won't) + V |
| Question | Am/Is/Are + S + going to + V? | Will + S + V? |

- Contractions: I'll, you'll, he'll, won't (= will not), gonna (informal spoken).
- Time words: tomorrow, next week, soon, this evening, in 2030.
- (TR: won't = will not. gonna sadece konuşma dilinde.)

## 3 classic traps

1. **will for fixed plans:** ❌ *I will meet my doctor tomorrow at 10.* (appointment) → ✅ *I'm going to meet my doctor tomorrow at 10.* (TR: Randevu gibi kesin plan → going to.)
2. **-ing after to:** ❌ *I'm going to studying.* → ✅ *I'm going to study.* (TR: to'dan sonra yalın fiil.)
3. **s on will:** ❌ *She wills come.* → ✅ *She will come.* (TR: will hiçbir kişide -s almaz.)

## 10 example sentences

1. I**'m going to visit** my grandmother tomorrow. (Yarın büyükannemi ziyaret edeceğim — plan.)
2. We**'re going to move** to a new flat. (Yeni bir daireye taşınacağız — karar verildi.)
3. Look at those clouds! It**'s going to rain**. (Bulutlara bak! Yağmur yağacak — belirti var.)
4. A: The bag is heavy. B: I**'ll help** you. (Anlık teklif → will.)
5. I**'ll call** you tonight, I promise. (Söz veriyorum → will.)
6. I think robots **will do** more jobs. (Genel tahmin → will.)
7. She **won't be** late. (Geç kalmayacak.)
8. **Will you come** with us? (Bizimle gelir misin?)
9. They**'re not going to buy** a car this year. (Bu yıl araba almayacaklar.)
10. One day people **will live** on Mars. (Bir gün insanlar Mars'ta yaşayacak.)

## Quick check

1. Fill in: Look! He ___ (fall)!
2. Choose: A: "I forgot my wallet." B: "I ___ (lend) you money." (will / am going to)
3. Correct the error: *She wills help us.*
4. Which fits a fixed plan: "I ___ see the dentist at 9 a.m."?
5. True or false: "I'm going to studying tonight." is correct.

<details>
<summary>Answers</summary>

1. is going to fall
2. will (’ll lend — instant offer)
3. She will help us.
4. ’m going to (fixed plan)
5. False — "I'm going to study tonight."
