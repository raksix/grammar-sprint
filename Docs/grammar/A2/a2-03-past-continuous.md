# Past Continuous

## Goal

- Describe actions in progress at a past moment and interrupted actions (when/while).
- (TR: Geçmişte süregelen eylemleri ve bölünen eylemleri anlatmak.)

## Rule in 60 seconds

- Form: **was/were + verb-ing**: I was sleeping, they were playing.
- Use it for: (a) action in progress at a past time, (b) a longer action cut by a short one.
- Interrupted action pattern: *I was cooking **when** he arrived.* / ***While** I was cooking, he arrived.*
- (TR: was/were + -ing. when kısa eylemden önce, while uzun eylemle kullanılır.)

## Forms

| Type | Structure | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Affirmative | S + was/were + V-ing | She was reading. |
| Negative | S + wasn't/weren't + V-ing | She wasn't reading. |
| Question | Was/Were + S + V-ing? | Was she reading? |
| Short answer | Yes, S + was / No, S + wasn't | Yes, she was. |

- was: I / he / she / it. were: you / we / they.
- Time anchors: at 8 p.m. yesterday, all day, this time last week, when…, while….
- Spelling of -ing: take → taking, run → running, study → studying.
- (TR: I/he/she/it ile was, diğerleriyle were. -ing yazım kuralları present continuous ile aynı.)

## 3 classic traps

1. **was/were mix:** ❌ *We was watching TV.* → ✅ *We were watching TV.* (TR: we/you/they ile were.)
2. **when vs while:** ❌ *While he arrived, I was cooking.* → ✅ *When he arrived, I was cooking.* (TR: Kısa bitmiş eylem when ile; while uzun süren eylemle.)
3. **Two long actions both continuous:** ❌ *I cooked while he watched.* (meaning both long) → ✅ *I was cooking while he was watching TV.* (TR: İki uzun eşzamanlı eylemde ikisi de continuous olur.)

## 10 example sentences

1. I **was sleeping** at 10 p.m. (Akşam 10'da uyuyordum.)
2. They **were playing** football all afternoon. (Bütün öğleden sonra futbol oynuyorlardı.)
3. She **was cooking** when I arrived. (Ben vardığımda yemek yapıyordu.)
4. **While** he **was driving**, it started to rain. (O araba sürerken yağmur başladı.)
5. We **weren't listening**. (Dinlemiyorduk.)
6. **Was it raining** this morning? (Bu sabah yağmur yağıyor muydu?)
7. I **was doing** my homework at 8 p.m. (Akşam 8'de ödevimi yapıyordum.)
8. The baby **was crying** loudly. (Bebek yüksek sesle ağlıyordu.)
9. **While** I **was waiting**, I read a magazine. (Beklerken bir dergi okudum.)
10. They **were laughing** during the film. (Film boyunca gülüyorlardı.)

## Quick check

1. Fill in: At 9 p.m. we ___ (watch) a match.
2. Correct the error: *You was sleeping.*
3. Join with when: I / cook + the phone / ring.
4. Choose: ___ (When / While) I was studying, my sister was playing.
5. True or false: "She was go to school." is correct.

<details>
<summary>Answers</summary>

1. were watching
2. You were sleeping.
3. I was cooking when the phone rang.
4. While
5. False — "She was going to school."

</details>
