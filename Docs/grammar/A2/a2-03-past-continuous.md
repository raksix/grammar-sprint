# Past Continuous

## Goal


- **Describe** actions in progress at a past moment and interrupted actions (when/while).
- **TR:** Geçmişte süregelen eylemleri ve bölünen eylemleri anlatmak.

- **Success check:** describe what you were doing at 9 p.m. yesterday.

- **Pair skill:** combine with past simple — I was cooking when he arrived.


## Rule in 60 seconds


- **Form:** **was/were + verb-ing**: I was sleeping, they were playing.
- **Use it for:** (a) action in progress at a past time, (b) a longer action cut by a short one.
- **Interrupted action pattern:** *I was cooking **when** he arrived.* / ***While** I was cooking, he arrived.*
- **TR:** was/were + -ing. when kısa eylemden önce, while uzun eylemle kullanılır.

- **Two uses:** an action in progress + an interrupted action.

- **when + short:** I was cooking when he arrived.

- **while + long:** While I was cooking, he arrived.


## Forms


| Type | Structure | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Affirmative | S + was/were + V-ing | She was reading. |
| Negative | S + wasn't/weren't + V-ing | She wasn't reading. |
| Question | Was/Were + S + V-ing? | Was she reading? |
| Short answer | Yes, S + was / No, S + wasn't | Yes, she was. |

- **was:** I / he / she / it. were: you / we / they.
- **Time anchors:** at 8 p.m. yesterday, all day, this time last week, when…, while….
- **Spelling of -ing:** take → taking, run → running, study → studying.
- **TR:** I/he/she/it ile was, diğerleriyle were. -ing yazım kuralları present continuous ile aynı.

- **Short answers:** Yes, she was. / No, she wasn't.

- **Word order:** the time phrase can open or close — At 8 p.m. I was cooking.

- **Short forms:** wasn't = was not; weren't = were not.


## 3 classic traps


- **was/were mix:** ❌ *We was watching TV.* → ✅ *We were watching TV.* (TR: we/you/they ile were.)
- **when vs while:** ❌ *While he arrived, I was cooking.* → ✅ *When he arrived, I was cooking.* (TR: Kısa bitmiş eylem when ile; while uzun süren eylemle.)
- **Two long actions both continuous:** ❌ *I cooked while he watched.* (meaning both long) → ✅ *I was cooking while he was watching TV.* (TR: İki uzun eşzamanlı eylemde ikisi de continuous olur.)


## 10 example sentences


- **I was sleeping at 10 p.m.** — Akşam 10'da uyuyordum.
- **They were playing football all afternoon.** — Bütün öğleden sonra futbol oynuyorlardı.
- **She was cooking when I arrived.** — Ben vardığımda yemek yapıyordu.
- **While he was driving, it started to rain.** — O araba sürerken yağmur başladı.
- **We weren't listening.** — Dinlemiyorduk.
- **Was it raining this morning?** — Bu sabah yağmur yağıyor muydu?
- **I was doing my homework at 8 p.m.** — Akşam 8'de ödevimi yapıyordum.
- **The baby was crying loudly.** — Bebek yüksek sesle ağlıyordu.
- **While I was waiting, I read a magazine.** — Beklerken bir dergi okudum.
- **They were laughing during the film.** — Film boyunca gülüyorlardı.


## Quick check


- **Q1:** Fill in: At 9 p.m. we ___ (watch) a match.
- **Q2:** Correct the error: *You was sleeping.*
- **Q3:** Join with when: I / cook + the phone / ring.
- **Q4:** Choose: ___ (When / While) I was studying, my sister was playing.
- **Q5:** True or false: "She was go to school." is correct.

- **Q6:** Choose: They ___ (was / were) playing.
<details>
<summary>Answers</summary>

- **A1:** were watching
- **A2:** You were sleeping.
- **A3:** I was cooking when the phone rang.
- **A4:** While
- **A5:** False — "She was going to school."

- **A6:** were.
</details>
