# Countable and Uncountable Nouns

## Goal

- Use **some/any, much/many, a lot of, how much/how many** correctly with countable and uncountable nouns.
- (TR: Sayılabilen ve sayılamayan isimlerle miktar ifadelerini doğru kullanmak.)

## Rule in 60 seconds

- Countable (apple/apples): singular + plural, use **a/an, many, few, how many**.
- Uncountable (water, rice, money, advice): no plural, no a/an; use **much, little, how much**.
- **some** (affirmative) / **any** (negative + question) and **a lot of** work with both.
- (TR: many/how many + sayılabilen çoğul; much/how much + sayılamayan. some olumlu, any olumsuz/soru.)

## Forms

| Word | Countable | Uncountable | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| many / how many / few | ✅ | ❌ | How many eggs? / few friends |
| much / how much / little | ❌ | ✅ | How much milk? / little sugar |
| some / any | ✅ | ✅ | some apples / some water; any apples / any water |
| a lot of / lots of | ✅ | ✅ | a lot of books / a lot of time |

- Common uncountables: water, milk, bread, rice, money, homework, advice, information, news, furniture, traffic, weather.
- Trick: some uncountables have countable "containers": a bottle of water, a piece of advice, a loaf of bread.
- (TR: money, news, advice, information sayılamaz — çoğul -s almaz, a/an almaz.)

## 3 classic traps

1. **Plural uncountable:** ❌ *informations / advices.* → ✅ *information / advice.* (TR: Bu kelimeler tekil kalır; "bir tavsiye" = a piece of advice.)
2. **much with countable:** ❌ *How much apples?* → ✅ *How many apples?* (TR: Sayılabilen çoğulla many/how many.)
3. **a/an with uncountable:** ❌ *a milk / a bread.* → ✅ *a glass of milk / a loaf of bread.* (TR: Sayılamayan isim tek başına a/an almaz.)

## 10 example sentences

1. How **many** students are there? (Kaç öğrenci var?)
2. How **much** sugar do you want? (Ne kadar şeker istersin?)
3. I have **a lot of** friends here. (Burada çok arkadaşım var.)
4. She doesn't have **much** time. (Pek zamanı yok.)
5. Are there **any** eggs left? (Hiç yumurta kaldı mı?)
6. There is **some** cheese in the fridge. (Dolapta biraz peynir var.)
7. He gave me good **advice**. (Bana iyi bir tavsiye verdi.)
8. The **news** is shocking. (Haberler şoke edici — tekil fiil!)
9. I bought **a bottle of** water and **a loaf of** bread. (Bir şişe su ve bir somun ekmek aldım.)
10. **Many** people like Turkish food. (Birçok kişi Türk yemeğini sever.)

## Quick check

1. Choose: How ___ (much / many) money do you need?
2. Correct the error: *She gave me two advices.*
3. Fill in: There aren't ___ (some / any) chairs.
4. True or false: "much friends" is correct.
5. Which is correct: "a news" or "a piece of news"?

<details>
<summary>Answers</summary>

1. much
2. She gave me two pieces of advice. (or: some advice)
3. any
4. False — "many friends"
5. a piece of news
