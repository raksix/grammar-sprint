# Countable and Uncountable Nouns

## Goal


- **Use** **some/any, much/many, a lot of, how much/how many** correctly with countable and uncountable nouns.
- **TR:** Sayılabilen ve sayılamayan isimlerle miktar ifadelerini doğru kullanmak.

- **Success check:** ask How many / How much questions about your fridge.


## Rule in 60 seconds


- **Countable (apple/apples):** singular + plural, use **a/an, many, few, how many**.
- **Uncountable (water, rice, money, advice):** no plural, no a/an; use **much, little, how much**.
- **some** (affirmative) / **any** (negative + question) and **a lot of** work with both.
- **TR:** many/how many + sayılabilen çoğul; much/how much + sayılamayan. some olumlu, any olumsuz/soru.

- **Plural test:** can you add -s? — apples yes, milks no.

- **a/an test:** a/an never sits before an uncountable noun.

- **some/any split:** some in + sentences; any in − and ? sentences.


## Forms


| Word | Countable | Uncountable | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| many / how many / few | ✅ | ❌ | How many eggs? / few friends |
| much / how much / little | ❌ | ✅ | How much milk? / little sugar |
| some / any | ✅ | ✅ | some apples / some water; any apples / any water |
| a lot of / lots of | ✅ | ✅ | a lot of books / a lot of time |

- **Common uncountables:** water, milk, bread, rice, money, homework, advice, information, news, furniture, traffic, weather.
- **Trick:** some uncountables have countable "containers": a bottle of water, a piece of advice, a loaf of bread.
- **TR:** money, news, advice, information sayılamaz — çoğul -s almaz, a/an almaz.

- **news is singular:** The news is shocking — never are.

- **few vs little:** few friends / little sugar.

- **No plural club:** furniture, weather and homework never take -s.


## 3 classic traps


- **Plural uncountable:** ❌ *informations / advices.* → ✅ *information / advice.* (TR: Bu kelimeler tekil kalır; "bir tavsiye" = a piece of advice.)
- **much with countable:** ❌ *How much apples?* → ✅ *How many apples?* (TR: Sayılabilen çoğulla many/how many.)
- **a/an with uncountable:** ❌ *a milk / a bread.* → ✅ *a glass of milk / a loaf of bread.* (TR: Sayılamayan isim tek başına a/an almaz.)


## 10 example sentences


- **How many students are there?** — Kaç öğrenci var?
- **How much sugar do you want?** — Ne kadar şeker istersin?
- **I have a lot of friends here.** — Burada çok arkadaşım var.
- **She doesn't have much time.** — Pek zamanı yok.
- **Are there any eggs left?** — Hiç yumurta kaldı mı?
- **There is some cheese in the fridge.** — Dolapta biraz peynir var.
- **He gave me good advice.** — Bana iyi bir tavsiye verdi.
- **The news is shocking.** — Haberler şoke edici — tekil fiil!
- **I bought a bottle of water and a loaf of bread.** — Bir şişe su ve bir somun ekmek aldım.
- **Many people like Turkish food.** — Birçok kişi Türk yemeğini sever.


## Quick check


- **Q1:** Choose: How ___ (much / many) money do you need?
- **Q2:** Correct the error: *She gave me two advices.*
- **Q3:** Fill in: There aren't ___ (some / any) chairs.
- **Q4:** True or false: "much friends" is correct.
- **Q5:** Which is correct: "a news" or "a piece of news"?

- **Q6:** Choose: There is ___ (some / any) milk left.
<details>
<summary>Answers</summary>

- **A1:** much
- **A2:** She gave me two pieces of advice. (or: some advice)
- **A3:** any
- **A4:** False — "many friends"
- **A5:** a piece of news

- **A6:** some.
</details>
