# Modals at A2: could, should, may, might, must, have to

## Goal

- Express ability, advice, possibility, and obligation with A2-level modals.
- (TR: Yetenek, tavsiye, olasılık ve zorunluluk için kip fiilleri kullanmak.)

## Rule in 60 seconds

- Modals never take -s and are followed by **bare infinitive**: She can swim, He must go.
- Negative: modal + not (can't, shouldn't, mustn't). Question: Modal + S + V? (Could you…?)
- **must** = strong personal obligation; **have to** = external rule; **mustn't** = prohibition (yasak).
- (TR: Modalden sonra fiil yalın gelir. mustn't = yasak, don't have to = gerek yok.)

## Forms

| Modal | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| can / could | ability, request, permission | She could swim at five. / Could you help me? |
| should / shouldn't | advice | You should rest. / You shouldn't hurry. |
| may / might | possibility, permission | It may rain. / May I come in? |
| must / mustn't | strong obligation / prohibition | You must wear a seatbelt. / You mustn't smoke here. |
| have to / don't have to | rule / no necessity | I have to wear a uniform. / You don't have to run. |

- Past of ability: could (I couldn't sleep). Advice regret: should have + V3 (B1, just recognise it).
- might = slightly less certain than may; both + base verb.
- (TR: could geçmiş yetenek; should tavsiye; may/might olasılık; must güçlü zorunluluk.)

## 3 classic traps

1. **to after modal:** ❌ *You must to go.* → ✅ *You must go.* (TR: Modalden sonra to gelmez — have to hariç.)
2. **mustn't vs don't have to:** ❌ *You don't have to smoke here.* (for a ban) → ✅ *You mustn't smoke here.* (TR: Yasak = mustn't; gereksizlik = don't have to.)
3. **-s on modal:** ❌ *He cans swim.* → ✅ *He can swim.* (TR: Modallar kişi eki almaz.)

## 10 example sentences

1. She **could** read at the age of four. (Dört yaşında okuyabiliyordu.)
2. **Could** you open the window, please? (Camı açar mısın lütfen?)
3. You **should** see a doctor. (Doktora görünmelisin.)
4. You **shouldn't** drink cold water. (Soğuk su içmemelisin.)
5. It **might** rain this evening. (Bu akşam yağmur yağabilir.)
6. **May** I use your phone? (Telefonunu kullanabilir miyim?)
7. You **must** stop at red lights. (Kırmızıda durmalısın.)
8. You **mustn't** park here. (Buraya park edemezsin — yasak.)
9. We **have to** be at school by 9. (Saat 9'a kadar okulda olmalıyız — kural.)
10. You **don't have to** bring food. (Yemek getirmene gerek yok.)

## Quick check

1. Fill in: You ___ (should / shouldn't) eat so fast. (advice: don't)
2. Choose for a ban: You ___ (mustn't / don't have to) touch that.
3. Correct the error: *He must to study.*
4. Which shows possibility: "It may snow" or "It must snow"?
5. True or false: "She cans drive." is correct.

<details>
<summary>Answers</summary>

1. shouldn't
2. mustn't
3. He must study.
4. "It may snow"
5. False — "She can drive."

</details>
