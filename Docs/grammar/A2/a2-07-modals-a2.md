# Modals at A2: could, should, may, might, must, have to

## Goal


- **Express** ability, advice, possibility, and obligation with A2-level modals.
- **TR:** Yetenek, tavsiye, olasılık ve zorunluluk için kip fiilleri kullanmak.

- **Success check:** give one advice, one rule and one ban aloud.

- **TR:** Bu derste must ile have to farkını öğreneceksin.


## Rule in 60 seconds


- **Modals** never take -s and are followed by **bare infinitive**: She can swim, He must go.
- **Negative:** modal + not (can't, shouldn't, mustn't). Question: Modal + S + V? (Could you…?)
- **must** = strong personal obligation; **have to** = external rule; **mustn't** = prohibition (yasak).
- **TR:** Modalden sonra fiil yalın gelir. mustn't = yasak, don't have to = gerek yok.

- **Bare verb rule:** can go and must go — never can to go.

- **Ban vs no need:** mustn't = yasak; don't have to = gerek yok.


## Forms


| Modal | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| can / could | ability, request, permission | She could swim at five. / Could you help me? |
| should / shouldn't | advice | You should rest. / You shouldn't hurry. |
| may / might | possibility, permission | It may rain. / May I come in? |
| must / mustn't | strong obligation / prohibition | You must wear a seatbelt. / You mustn't smoke here. |
| have to / don't have to | rule / no necessity | I have to wear a uniform. / You don't have to run. |

- **Past of ability:** could (I couldn't sleep). Advice regret: should have + V3 (B1, just recognise it).
- **might** = slightly less certain than may; both + base verb.
- **TR:** could geçmiş yetenek; should tavsiye; may/might olasılık; must güçlü zorunluluk.

- **Permission pair:** May I come in? / Could you help me?

- **Advice form:** You should rest. / You shouldn't hurry.

- **Obligation pair:** must = my rule; have to = their rule.


## 3 classic traps


- **to after modal:** ❌ *You must to go.* → ✅ *You must go.* (TR: Modalden sonra to gelmez — have to hariç.)
- **mustn't vs don't have to:** ❌ *You don't have to smoke here.* (for a ban) → ✅ *You mustn't smoke here.* (TR: Yasak = mustn't; gereksizlik = don't have to.)
- **-s on modal:** ❌ *He cans swim.* → ✅ *He can swim.* (TR: Modallar kişi eki almaz.)


## 10 example sentences


- **She could read at the age of four.** — Dört yaşında okuyabiliyordu.
- **Could you open the window, please?** — Camı açar mısın lütfen?
- **You should see a doctor.** — Doktora görünmelisin.
- **You shouldn't drink cold water.** — Soğuk su içmemelisin.
- **It might rain this evening.** — Bu akşam yağmur yağabilir.
- **May I use your phone?** — Telefonunu kullanabilir miyim?
- **You must stop at red lights.** — Kırmızıda durmalısın.
- **You mustn't park here.** — Buraya park edemezsin — yasak.
- **We have to be at school by 9.** — Saat 9'a kadar okulda olmalıyız — kural.
- **You don't have to bring food.** — Yemek getirmene gerek yok.


## Quick check


- **Q1:** Fill in: You ___ (should / shouldn't) eat so fast. (advice: don't)
- **Q2:** Choose for a ban: You ___ (mustn't / don't have to) touch that.
- **Q3:** Correct the error: *He must to study.*
- **Q4:** Which shows possibility: "It may snow" or "It must snow"?
- **Q5:** True or false: "She cans drive." is correct.

- **Q6:** Choose for no need: You ___ (mustn't / don't have to) pay now.
<details>
<summary>Answers</summary>

- **A1:** shouldn't
- **A2:** mustn't
- **A3:** He must study.
- **A4:** "It may snow"
- **A5:** False — "She can drive."

- **A6:** don't have to.
</details>
