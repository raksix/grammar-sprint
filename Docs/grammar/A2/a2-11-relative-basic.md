# Relative Clauses (Basic): who / which / that

## Goal


- **Join** two sentences with defining relative clauses using **who** (people), **which** (things), **that** (both).
- **TR:** who/which/that ile iki cümleyi tek cümlede birleştirmek.

- **Success check:** join two sentences about your family with who.


## Rule in 60 seconds


- **who** = people: *the man who lives next door.*
- **which** = things/animals: *the book which I bought.*
- **that** = people OR things (informal, common): *the film that we watched.*
- **The** relative clause comes **directly after** the noun it describes.
- **TR:** who insanlar, which eşya/hayvan, that ikisi için de. İlgi cümlesi ismin hemen arkasına gelir.

- **Comma rule:** A2 defining clauses take no commas.


## Forms


| Pattern | Structure | Example |
|---|---|---|
| who + verb | noun (person) + who + verb | The girl **who called** is my sister. |
| which + verb | noun (thing) + which + verb | The car **which broke down** is mine. |
| that + verb | noun + that + verb | The film **that won** was great. |
| who(m) + subject + verb | noun + who/that + subj + verb | The man **who I met** is a doctor. |
| which + subject + verb | noun + which/that + subj + verb | The book **which I read** was long. |
| negative relative | noun + who/which/that + don't/doesn't… | A shop **that doesn't open** Sundays. |

- **No extra subject pronoun:** ❌ *the man who he came* → ✅ *the man who came*.
- **A2** level = **defining** clauses only (no commas): they tell WHICH one.
- **TR:** Tanımlayan cümlecikte virgül yok: the house that I bought. Fazladan zamir koyma.
- **"whose"** (possessive) is B1 — at A2 just recognise it: *the boy whose bag…*.
- **"where" for places appears here as preview:** *the city where I was born.*
- **Omitting** the pronoun (object position) is B1 preview: *the book (that) I bought.*
- **which vs that in defining clauses:** both OK at A2 — *the car which/that broke down.*
- **TR:** Nesne konumunda that düşebilir — detayı B1'de: the book I bought.

- **Object drop preview:** the book (that) I bought — full rule at B1.


## 3 classic traps


- **who for things:** ❌ *the book who I like* → ✅ *the book which/that I like.* (TR: Eşya için who olmaz.)
- **Double subject:** ❌ *the woman who she works here* → ✅ *the woman who works here.* (TR: who'dan sonra tekrar she/he koyma.)
- **Commas in defining clauses:** ❌ *The man, who came, is my uncle.* (A2) → ✅ *The man who came is my uncle.* (TR: A2'de tanımlayan cümlecikte virgül yok; virgüllü yapı B1'dir.)


## 10 example sentences


- **The man who lives next door is a teacher.** — Yan kapıda yaşayan adam öğretmendir.
- **The woman who called you is my aunt.** — Seni arayan kadın halamdır.
- **The book which I bought is very interesting.** — Aldığım kitap çok ilginç.
- **The film that we watched was fantastic.** — İzlediğimiz film harikaydı.
- **That's the restaurant that serves cheap pizza.** — Ucuz pizza servis eden restoran işte bu.
- **People who smoke here must pay a fine.** — Burada sigara içenler ceza öder.
- **The bag which she lost was black.** — Kaybettiği çanta siyahtı.
- **I know a shop that sells old coins.** — Eski para satan bir dükkân biliyorum.
- **The dog that barks all night is ours.** — Bütün gece havlayan köpek bizimdir.
- **She is the friend who helped me yesterday.** — Dün bana yardım eden arkadaş işte bu.


## Quick check


- **Q1:** Fill in: The boy ___ sits next to me is Ali.
- **Q2:** Fill in: The phone ___ I bought is slow.
- **Q3:** Correct the error: *The car who is red is mine.*
- **Q4:** Correct the error: *The girl who she sings is famous.*
- **Q5:** True or false: "The house that Jack built" is a defining clause with no commas.
- **Q6:** Join: "The man is my uncle. He came yesterday." (use who)

<details>
<summary>Answers</summary>

- **A1:** who / that (person → who)
- **A2:** which / that (thing → which or that)
- **A3:** The car which/that is red is mine. (things → which/that)
- **A4:** The girl who sings is famous. (no double subject)
- **A5:** True.
- **A6:** The man who came yesterday is my uncle.

</details>
