# Reported Speech (Basic): say / tell Statements

## Goal

- Report statements with **say** and **tell**, using basic backshift (present → past) and time-word changes.
- (TR: say/tell ile cümle aktarmak; basit zaman kayması ve zaman sözcüğü değişimleri.)

## Rule in 60 seconds

- **say** (+ to me) vs **tell me/him/her**: *He said (that)… / He told me (that)…*
- Backshift intro: present simple → past simple: *"I work" → He said he worked.*
- am/is/are → was/were; have/has → had; will → would.
- Time changes: *today → that day, now → then, yesterday → the day before.*
- (TR: say'den sonra nesne yok; tell'den sonra nesne şart: told me. Zaman bir adım geriye: work → worked, will → would.)

## Forms

| Pattern | Structure | Example |
|---|---|---|
| say statement | He said (that) + clause | He **said (that) he was** tired. |
| tell statement | He told me (that) + clause | He **told me (that) he was** tired. |
| present → past | "I like" → said liked | "I like tea" → She **said she liked** tea. |
| be backshift | am/is → was; are → were | "I am busy" → He **said he was** busy. |
| will → would | "I will" → said would | "I'll call" → She **said she would** call. |
| time change | yesterday → the day before | "I came yesterday" → He **said he had come the day before**. |

- **that** after said/told is optional: *He said he was ill = He said that he was ill.*
- ❌ *He said me…* → ✅ *He said to me… / He told me…*
- (TR: said me yanlış; told me doğru. that yazmak zorunlu değil.)
- Pronouns shift too: *I → he/she, my → his/her, here → there.*
- A2 = **statements only**; questions/commands come at B1.
- Past simple → past perfect (intro only): *"I saw" → said she had seen.*
- can → could (intro): *"I can swim" → He said he could swim.*
- (TR: A2'de sadece düz cümle aktarımı; soru/emir B1'de. Görülen zaman bir adım geriye.)
- If the report is still true now, backshift is still safer at A2: *She said she liked tea.*

## 3 classic traps

1. **said + object:** ❌ *He said me he was ill.* → ✅ *He said he was ill. / He told me he was ill.* (TR: said + nesne olmaz; tell + nesne olur.)
2. **No backshift:** ❌ *"I work" → He said he works.* → ✅ *He said he worked.* (TR: Aktarımda zaman bir adım geriye kayar.)
3. **tell without object:** ❌ *She told that she was late.* → ✅ *She told me/us that she was late.* (TR: tell'den sonra kime söylediğin yazılır.)

## 10 example sentences

1. "I am tired." → He **said he was** tired. ("Yorgunum" → Yorgun olduğunu söyledi.)
2. "I like coffee." → She **said she liked** coffee. (Kahve sevdiğini söyledi.)
3. "We will come." → They **said they would** come. (Geleceklerini söylediler.)
4. "I have a car." → He **said he had** a car. (Arabası olduğunu söyledi.)
5. She **told me** she was busy. (Meşgul olduğunu söyledi.)
6. He **told us** he would help. (Yardım edeceğini söyledi.)
7. "I saw her yesterday." → She **said she had seen her the day before**. (Onu dün gördüğünü söyledi.)
8. "I am working now." → He **said he was working then**. (O sırada çalıştığını söyledi.)
9. They **said** the film was great. (Filmin harika olduğunu söylediler.)
10. Mum **told me** dinner was ready. (Annem yemeğin hazır olduğunu söyledi.)

## Quick check

1. Correct the error: *She said me she was happy.*
2. Report: "I want tea." → She said ___.
3. Report: "I will help." → He told me ___.
4. Fill the time change: "yesterday" → ___ in reported speech.
5. True or false: "He told that he was ill" is correct.
6. Report: "I can swim." → He said ___.

<details>
<summary>Answers</summary>

1. She said she was happy. / She told me she was happy.
2. She said (that) she wanted tea.
3. He told me (that) he would help.
4. the day before / the previous day
5. False — tell needs an object: He told me (that) he was ill.
6. He said (that) he could swim.

</details>
