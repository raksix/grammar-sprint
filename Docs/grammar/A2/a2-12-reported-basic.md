# Reported Speech (Basic): say / tell Statements

## Goal


- **Report** statements with **say** and **tell**, using basic backshift (present → past) and time-word changes.
- **TR:** say/tell ile cümle aktarmak; basit zaman kayması ve zaman sözcüğü değişimleri.

- **Success check:** report two things a friend said yesterday.


## Rule in 60 seconds


- **say** (+ to me) vs **tell me/him/her**: *He said (that)… / He told me (that)…*
- **Backshift intro:** present simple → past simple: *"I work" → He said he worked.*
- **am/is/are** → was/were; have/has → had; will → would.
- **Time changes:** *today → that day, now → then, yesterday → the day before.*
- **TR:** say'den sonra nesne yok; tell'den sonra nesne şart: told me. Zaman bir adım geriye: work → worked, will → would.

- **that is free:** He said he was ill = He said that he was ill.


## Forms


| Pattern | Structure | Example |
|---|---|---|
| say statement | He said (that) + clause | He **said (that) he was** tired. |
| tell statement | He told me (that) + clause | He **told me (that) he was** tired. |
| present → past | "I like" → said liked | "I like tea" → She **said she liked** tea. |
| be backshift | am/is → was; are → were | "I am busy" → He **said he was** busy. |
| will → would | "I will" → said would | "I'll call" → She **said she would** call. |
| time change | yesterday → the day before | "I came yesterday" → He **said he had come the day before**. |

- **that** after said/told is optional: *He said he was ill = He said that he was ill.*
- ❌ *He said me…* → ✅ *He said to me… / He told me…*
- **TR:** said me yanlış; told me doğru. that yazmak zorunlu değil.
- **Pronouns shift too:** *I → he/she, my → his/her, here → there.*
- **A2** = **statements only**; questions/commands come at B1.
- **Past simple → past perfect (intro only):** *"I saw" → said she had seen.*
- **can → could (intro):** *"I can swim" → He said he could swim.*
- **TR:** A2'de sadece düz cümle aktarımı; soru/emir B1'de. Görülen zaman bir adım geriye.
- **If** the report is still true now, backshift is still safer at A2: *She said she liked tea.*

- **say/tell drill:** said + clause; told + person + clause.


## 3 classic traps


- **said + object:** ❌ *He said me he was ill.* → ✅ *He said he was ill. / He told me he was ill.* (TR: said + nesne olmaz; tell + nesne olur.)
- **No backshift:** ❌ *"I work" → He said he works.* → ✅ *He said he worked.* (TR: Aktarımda zaman bir adım geriye kayar.)
- **tell without object:** ❌ *She told that she was late.* → ✅ *She told me/us that she was late.* (TR: tell'den sonra kime söylediğin yazılır.)


## 10 example sentences


- **"I am tired." → He said he was tired.** — "Yorgunum" → Yorgun olduğunu söyledi.
- **"I like coffee." → She said she liked coffee.** — Kahve sevdiğini söyledi.
- **"We will come." → They said they would come.** — Geleceklerini söylediler.
- **"I have a car." → He said he had a car.** — Arabası olduğunu söyledi.
- **She told me she was busy.** — Meşgul olduğunu söyledi.
- **He told us he would help.** — Yardım edeceğini söyledi.
- **"I saw her yesterday." → She said she had seen her the day before.** — Onu dün gördüğünü söyledi.
- **"I am working now." → He said he was working then.** — O sırada çalıştığını söyledi.
- **They said the film was great.** — Filmin harika olduğunu söylediler.
- **Mum told me dinner was ready.** — Annem yemeğin hazır olduğunu söyledi.


## Quick check


- **Q1:** Correct the error: *She said me she was happy.*
- **Q2:** Report: "I want tea." → She said ___.
- **Q3:** Report: "I will help." → He told me ___.
- **Q4:** Fill the time change: "yesterday" → ___ in reported speech.
- **Q5:** True or false: "He told that he was ill" is correct.
- **Q6:** Report: "I can swim." → He said ___.

<details>
<summary>Answers</summary>

- **A1:** She said she was happy. / She told me she was happy.
- **A2:** She said (that) she wanted tea.
- **A3:** He told me (that) he would help.
- **A4:** the day before / the previous day
- **A5:** False — tell needs an object: He told me (that) he was ill.
- **A6:** He said (that) he could swim.

</details>
