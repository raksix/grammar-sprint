# Comparatives and Superlatives

## Goal

- Compare two things (**-er / more … than**) and describe extremes (**the -est / the most**).
- (TR: İki şeyi karşılaştırmak ve en üstünlük bildirmek.)

## Rule in 60 seconds

- Short adjectives (1 syllable): + **-er / -est**: tall → taller → tallest. Use **than** after comparatives, **the** before superlatives.
- Long adjectives (2+ syllables): **more / most**: expensive → more expensive → most expensive.
- Equality: **as + adj + as**: She is as tall as me.
- (TR: Kısa sıfat -er/-est; uzun sıfat more/most. Karşılaştırmada than, superlative'ta the.)

## Forms

| Pattern | Structure | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Comparative (short) | adj-er + than | Ali is taller than me. |
| Superlative (short) | the + adj-est | Ali is the tallest. |
| Comparative (long) | more + adj + than | This is more expensive than that. |
| Superlative (long) | the + most + adj | This is the most expensive. |
| Equality | as + adj + as | It is as big as mine. |

- Spelling: big → bigger/biggest; happy → happier/happiest; large → larger/largest.
- Irregulars: good → better → best; bad → worse → worst; far → farther/further → farthest/furthest.
- Two-syllable adjectives ending in -y, -er, -ow often take -er/-est: happy, clever, narrow.
- (TR: good/better/best ve bad/worse/worst ezberlenir; -y ile bitenler -ier/-iest olur.)

## 3 classic traps

1. **Double comparative:** ❌ *more taller.* → ✅ *taller.* (TR: Ya -er ya more, ikisi birden olmaz.)
2. **Missing than/the:** ❌ *She is taller me.* → ✅ *She is taller than me.* (TR: Comparative'ta than, superlative'ta the şart.)
3. **then vs than:** ❌ *bigger then.* → ✅ *bigger than.* (TR: Karşılaştırma than ile yazılır, then = sonra.)

## 10 example sentences

1. My flat is **bigger than** yours. (Dairem seninkinden büyük.)
2. This exam was **easier than** the last one. (Bu sınav sonuncusundan kolaydı.)
3. She is **the tallest** in our class. (Sınıfımızın en uzunu.)
4. This is **the most interesting** book I have. (Bu sahip olduğum en ilginç kitap.)
5. Today is **colder than** yesterday. (Bugün dünden soğuk.)
6. My bag is **as heavy as** yours. (Çantam seninki kadar ağır.)
7. Health is **better than** money. (Sağlık paradan iyidir.)
8. That was **the worst** film ever. (Şimdiye kadarki en kötü filmdi.)
9. English is **easier for me than** maths. (İngilizce benim için matematikten kolay.)
10. Everest is **the highest** mountain in the world. (Everest dünyadaki en yüksek dağdır.)

## Quick check

1. Fill in: A car is ___ (fast) than a bike.
2. Correct the error: *This is more cheaper.*
3. Write the superlative: This puzzle is ___ (difficult) one.
4. Choose: She is ___ (as tall as / taller as) her sister.
5. True or false: "He is the most tallest student." is correct.

<details>
<summary>Answers</summary>

1. faster
2. This is cheaper.
3. the most difficult
4. as tall as
5. False — "He is the tallest student."

</details>
