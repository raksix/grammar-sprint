# Comparatives and Superlatives

## Goal


- **Compare** two things (**-er / more … than**) and describe extremes (**the -est / the most**).
- **TR:** İki şeyi karşılaştırmak ve en üstünlük bildirmek.

- **Success check:** compare two cities using -er and the most.


## Rule in 60 seconds


- **Short adjectives (1 syllable):** + **-er / -est**: tall → taller → tallest. Use **than** after comparatives, **the** before superlatives.
- **Long adjectives (2+ syllables):** **more / most**: expensive → more expensive → most expensive.
- **Equality:** **as + adj + as**: She is as tall as me.
- **TR:** Kısa sıfat -er/-est; uzun sıfat more/most. Karşılaştırmada than, superlative'ta the.

- **Syllable test:** tall (1) → taller; expensive (3) → more expensive.

- **the + than:** superlative takes the; comparative takes than.

- **Equality slot:** as tall as — no -er and no more.


## Forms


| Pattern | Structure | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Comparative (short) | adj-er + than | Ali is taller than me. |
| Superlative (short) | the + adj-est | Ali is the tallest. |
| Comparative (long) | more + adj + than | This is more expensive than that. |
| Superlative (long) | the + most + adj | This is the most expensive. |
| Equality | as + adj + as | It is as big as mine. |

- **Spelling:** big → bigger/biggest; happy → happier/happiest; large → larger/largest.
- **Irregulars:** good → better → best; bad → worse → worst; far → farther/further → farthest/furthest.
- **Two-syllable** adjectives ending in -y, -er, -ow often take -er/-est: happy, clever, narrow.
- **TR:** good/better/best ve bad/worse/worst ezberlenir; -y ile bitenler -ier/-iest olur.

- **-y rule:** happy → happier; -e rule: large → larger.

- **than vs then:** comparison = than; time order = then.


## 3 classic traps


- **Double comparative:** ❌ *more taller.* → ✅ *taller.* (TR: Ya -er ya more, ikisi birden olmaz.)
- **Missing than/the:** ❌ *She is taller me.* → ✅ *She is taller than me.* (TR: Comparative'ta than, superlative'ta the şart.)
- **then vs than:** ❌ *bigger then.* → ✅ *bigger than.* (TR: Karşılaştırma than ile yazılır, then = sonra.)


## 10 example sentences


- **My flat is bigger than yours.** — Dairem seninkinden büyük.
- **This exam was easier than the last one.** — Bu sınav sonuncusundan kolaydı.
- **She is the tallest in our class.** — Sınıfımızın en uzunu.
- **This is the most interesting book I have.** — Bu sahip olduğum en ilginç kitap.
- **Today is colder than yesterday.** — Bugün dünden soğuk.
- **My bag is as heavy as yours.** — Çantam seninki kadar ağır.
- **Health is better than money.** — Sağlık paradan iyidir.
- **That was the worst film ever.** — Şimdiye kadarki en kötü filmdi.
- **English is easier for me than maths.** — İngilizce benim için matematikten kolay.
- **Everest is the highest mountain in the world.** — Everest dünyadaki en yüksek dağdır.


## Quick check


- **Q1:** Fill in: A car is ___ (fast) than a bike.
- **Q2:** Correct the error: *This is more cheaper.*
- **Q3:** Write the superlative: This puzzle is ___ (difficult) one.
- **Q4:** Choose: She is ___ (as tall as / taller as) her sister.
- **Q5:** True or false: "He is the most tallest student." is correct.

- **Q6:** Write the comparative and superlative of: big.
<details>
<summary>Answers</summary>

- **A1:** faster
- **A2:** This is cheaper.
- **A3:** the most difficult
- **A4:** as tall as
- **A5:** False — "He is the tallest student."

- **A6:** bigger — biggest.
</details>
