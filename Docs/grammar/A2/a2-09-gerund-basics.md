# Gerund Basics: verb + -ing vs Infinitive

## Goal


- **Know** which verbs take **-ing** (like, enjoy, finish) and which take **to** (want, need, would like).
- **TR:** Hangi fiillerin -ing, hangilerinin to aldığını bilmek.

- **Success check:** say two -ing likes and two to wants about yourself.

- **TR:** Bu derste edatlardan sonra -ing kuralını öğreneceksin.


## Rule in 60 seconds


- **-ing group:** like, love, hate, enjoy, finish, mind, keep, practise, avoid.
- **to group:** want, need, decide, plan, hope, try, learn, would like.
- **After** prepositions always **-ing**: good at swimming, afraid of flying.
- **TR:** Edatlardan sonra hep -ing: interested in reading. want/need/decide + to.

- **-ing list:** like, love, hate, enjoy, finish, mind, keep and avoid.

- **to list:** want, need, decide, plan, hope, learn and would like.

- **Prep rule:** a preposition never takes to + verb — always -ing.


## Forms


| Pattern | Structure | Example |
|---|---|---|
| verb + -ing | enjoy/like/finish + V-ing | I enjoy swimming. |
| verb + to | want/need/decide + to V | I want to swim. |
| would like + to | ’d like + to V | I'd like to swim. |
| prep + -ing | prep + V-ing | She is good at drawing. |

- **like** + -ing (general pleasure) vs would like + to (specific wish now): I like tea / I'd like a tea.
- **go + -ing for activities:** go shopping, go swimming, go hiking.
- **TR:** like + -ing genel zevk; would like + to şu anki istek. Aktiviteler: go swimming.

- **Tea test:** I like tea (general) / I'd like a tea (now).

- **Activity go:** go shopping, go swimming and go hiking.


## 3 classic traps


- **want + -ing:** ❌ *I want going.* → ✅ *I want to go.* (TR: want/need + to alır.)
- **Preposition + to-verb:** ❌ *I'm good at to draw.* → ✅ *I'm good at drawing.* (TR: Edattan sonra -ing.)
- **would like + -ing:** ❌ *I'd like going now.* → ✅ *I'd like to go now.* (TR: would like + to.)


## 10 example sentences


- **I like reading novels.** — Roman okumayı severim.
- **She enjoys cooking.** — Yemek yapmaktan keyif alır.
- **They finished cleaning at 8.** — Saat 8'de temizliği bitirdiler.
- **I want to learn Spanish.** — İspanyolca öğrenmek istiyorum.
- **We need to hurry.** — Acele etmemiz gerek.
- **He decided to stay home.** — Evde kalmaya karar verdi.
- **I'd like to drink tea.** — Çay içmek isterim — şu anki istek.
- **She is afraid of flying.** — Uçmaktan korkar.
- **Let's go shopping on Saturday.** — Cumartesi alışverişe gidelim.
- **Keep trying — you will pass.** — Denemeye devam et — geçeceksin.


## Quick check


- **Q1:** Fill in: She enjoys ___ (dance).
- **Q2:** Fill in: They want ___ (visit) us.
- **Q3:** Correct the error: *He is interested in to play chess.*
- **Q4:** Choose: "I'd like ___ tea." (a tea / to tea → write full form)
- **Q5:** True or false: "I need going home." is correct.

- **Q6:** Fill in: He avoids ___ (drive) at night.
<details>
<summary>Answers</summary>

- **A1:** dancing
- **A2:** to visit
- **A3:** He is interested in playing chess.
- **A4:** I'd like to drink tea. (would like + to + verb)
- **A5:** False — "I need to go home."

- **A6:** driving.
</details>
