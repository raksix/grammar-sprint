# Gerund Basics: verb + -ing vs Infinitive

## Goal

- Know which verbs take **-ing** (like, enjoy, finish) and which take **to** (want, need, would like).
- (TR: Hangi fiillerin -ing, hangilerinin to aldığını bilmek.)

## Rule in 60 seconds

- **-ing group:** like, love, hate, enjoy, finish, mind, keep, practise, avoid.
- **to group:** want, need, decide, plan, hope, try, learn, would like.
- After prepositions always **-ing**: good at swimming, afraid of flying.
- (TR: Edatlardan sonra hep -ing: interested in reading. want/need/decide + to.)

## Forms

| Pattern | Structure | Example |
|---|---|---|
| verb + -ing | enjoy/like/finish + V-ing | I enjoy swimming. |
| verb + to | want/need/decide + to V | I want to swim. |
| would like + to | ’d like + to V | I'd like to swim. |
| prep + -ing | prep + V-ing | She is good at drawing. |

- like + -ing (general pleasure) vs would like + to (specific wish now): I like tea / I'd like a tea.
- go + -ing for activities: go shopping, go swimming, go hiking.
- (TR: like + -ing genel zevk; would like + to şu anki istek. Aktiviteler: go swimming.)

## 3 classic traps

1. **want + -ing:** ❌ *I want going.* → ✅ *I want to go.* (TR: want/need + to alır.)
2. **Preposition + to-verb:** ❌ *I'm good at to draw.* → ✅ *I'm good at drawing.* (TR: Edattan sonra -ing.)
3. **would like + -ing:** ❌ *I'd like going now.* → ✅ *I'd like to go now.* (TR: would like + to.)

## 10 example sentences

1. I **like reading** novels. (Roman okumayı severim.)
2. She **enjoys cooking**. (Yemek yapmaktan keyif alır.)
3. They **finished cleaning** at 8. (Saat 8'de temizliği bitirdiler.)
4. I **want to learn** Spanish. (İspanyolca öğrenmek istiyorum.)
5. We **need to hurry**. (Acele etmemiz gerek.)
6. He decided **to stay** home. (Evde kalmaya karar verdi.)
7. I**'d like to drink** tea. (Çay içmek isterim — şu anki istek.)
8. She is afraid **of flying**. (Uçmaktan korkar.)
9. Let's **go shopping** on Saturday. (Cumartesi alışverişe gidelim.)
10. Keep **trying** — you will pass. (Denemeye devam et — geçeceksin.)

## Quick check

1. Fill in: She enjoys ___ (dance).
2. Fill in: They want ___ (visit) us.
3. Correct the error: *He is interested in to play chess.*
4. Choose: "I'd like ___ tea." (a tea / to tea → write full form)
5. True or false: "I need going home." is correct.

<details>
<summary>Answers</summary>

1. dancing
2. to visit
3. He is interested in playing chess.
4. I'd like to drink tea. (would like + to + verb)
5. False — "I need to go home."

</details>
