# Past Simple: Irregular Verbs

## Goal


- **Use the top irregular past forms correctly:** go → went, eat → ate, see → saw.
- **TR:** En sık kullanılan düzensiz fiillerin geçmiş hallerini doğru kullanmak.

- **Success check:** say went, ate, saw, bought and took without pausing.


## Rule in 60 seconds


- **Irregular** verbs do **not** take -ed; each has its own past form: buy → bought, drink → drank, write → wrote.
- **Negative** and question still use **did + base verb**: didn't go, Did you eat?
- **Never** add -ed to an irregular past form.
- **TR:** Düzensiz fiilin geçmiş hali ezberlenir. Olumsuz ve soruda did + yalın fiil kullanılır.

- **No -ed rule:** goed, eated and wented do not exist.

- **Helper did:** Did you go? — never Did you went?

- **Memory trick:** learn irregulars in small groups of five.


## Forms


| Type | Structure | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Affirmative | S + irregular past | She went home. |
| Negative | S + didn't + base V | She didn't go home. |
| Question | Did + S + base V? | Did she go home? |
| Short answer | Yes, S + did / No, S + didn't | Yes, she did. |

- **be** → was/were; have → had; do → did; go → went; get → got
- **see** → saw; come → came; take → took; make → made; give → gave
- **eat** → ate; drink → drank; write → wrote; read → read (/red/); speak → spoke
- **think** → thought; buy → bought; bring → brought; find → found; lose → lost
- **Note:** **read** is spelled the same, pronounced /red/ in the past.
- **TR:** Önce bu 20 fiili ezberle; read yazımı aynı kalır ama /red/ okunur.

- **was/were:** I, he, she, it + was; you, we, they + were.

- **Short answers:** Yes, she did. / No, she didn't.


## 3 classic traps


- **-ed on irregulars:** ❌ *I goed / eated.* → ✅ *I went / ate.* (TR: Düzensiz fiile -ed gelmez.)
- **Past form after did:** ❌ *Did you went?* → ✅ *Did you go?* (TR: did varsa fiil yalın olur.)
- **was/were confusion:** ❌ *They was happy.* → ✅ *They were happy.* (TR: I/he/she/it + was; you/we/they + were.)


## 10 example sentences


- **I went to Ankara last month.** — Geçen ay Ankara'ya gittim.
- **She ate breakfast at 7 a.m.** — Sabah 7'de kahvaltı etti.
- **We saw an old friend yesterday.** — Dün eski bir arkadaş gördük.
- **He bought a new jacket.** — Yeni bir ceket aldı.
- **They came home late.** — Eve geç geldiler.
- **I didn't take the bus.** — Otobüse binmedim.
- **She didn't write back.** — Cevap yazmadı.
- **Did you drink your coffee?** — Kahveni içtin mi?
- **Did he find his keys?** — Anahtarlarını buldu mu?
- **The children had fun and read comics.** — Çocuklar eğlendi ve çizgi roman okudu.


## Quick check


- **Q1:** Fill in: She ___ (go) to school by bus yesterday.
- **Q2:** Correct the error: *Did you bought milk?*
- **Q3:** Write the past forms: eat, see, take, think, lose.
- **Q4:** Choose: They ___ (was / were) at home last night.
- **Q5:** True or false: "I didn't saw him." is correct.

- **Q6:** Write the past of: go, buy, find.
<details>
<summary>Answers</summary>

- **A1:** went
- **A2:** Did you buy milk?
- **A3:** ate, saw, took, thought, lost
- **A4:** were
- **A5:** False — "I didn't see him."

- **A6:** went, bought, found.
</details>
