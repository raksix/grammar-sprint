# Past Simple: Irregular Verbs

## Goal

- Use the top irregular past forms correctly: go → went, eat → ate, see → saw.
- (TR: En sık kullanılan düzensiz fiillerin geçmiş hallerini doğru kullanmak.)

## Rule in 60 seconds

- Irregular verbs do **not** take -ed; each has its own past form: buy → bought, drink → drank, write → wrote.
- Negative and question still use **did + base verb**: didn't go, Did you eat?
- Never add -ed to an irregular past form.
- (TR: Düzensiz fiilin geçmiş hali ezberlenir. Olumsuz ve soruda did + yalın fiil kullanılır.)

## Forms

| Type | Structure | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Affirmative | S + irregular past | She went home. |
| Negative | S + didn't + base V | She didn't go home. |
| Question | Did + S + base V? | Did she go home? |
| Short answer | Yes, S + did / No, S + didn't | Yes, she did. |

- Top-20 irregulars to memorise first:
  - be → was/were; have → had; do → did; go → went; get → got
  - see → saw; come → came; take → took; make → made; give → gave
  - eat → ate; drink → drank; write → wrote; read → read (/red/); speak → spoke
  - think → thought; buy → bought; bring → brought; find → found; lose → lost
- Note: **read** is spelled the same, pronounced /red/ in the past.
- (TR: Önce bu 20 fiili ezberle; read yazımı aynı kalır ama /red/ okunur.)

## 3 classic traps

1. **-ed on irregulars:** ❌ *I goed / eated.* → ✅ *I went / ate.* (TR: Düzensiz fiile -ed gelmez.)
2. **Past form after did:** ❌ *Did you went?* → ✅ *Did you go?* (TR: did varsa fiil yalın olur.)
3. **was/were confusion:** ❌ *They was happy.* → ✅ *They were happy.* (TR: I/he/she/it + was; you/we/they + were.)

## 10 example sentences

1. I **went** to Ankara last month. (Geçen ay Ankara'ya gittim.)
2. She **ate** breakfast at 7 a.m. (Sabah 7'de kahvaltı etti.)
3. We **saw** an old friend yesterday. (Dün eski bir arkadaş gördük.)
4. He **bought** a new jacket. (Yeni bir ceket aldı.)
5. They **came** home late. (Eve geç geldiler.)
6. I **didn't take** the bus. (Otobüse binmedim.)
7. She **didn't write** back. (Cevap yazmadı.)
8. **Did you drink** your coffee? (Kahveni içtin mi?)
9. **Did he find** his keys? (Anahtarlarını buldu mu?)
10. The children **had** fun and **read** comics. (Çocuklar eğlendi ve çizgi roman okudu.)

## Quick check

1. Fill in: She ___ (go) to school by bus yesterday.
2. Correct the error: *Did you bought milk?*
3. Write the past forms: eat, see, take, think, lose.
4. Choose: They ___ (was / were) at home last night.
5. True or false: "I didn't saw him." is correct.

<details>
<summary>Answers</summary>

1. went
2. Did you buy milk?
3. ate, saw, took, thought, lost
4. were
5. False — "I didn't see him."

</details>
